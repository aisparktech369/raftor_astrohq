# Tarot

A page at `/services/tarot`, open to anyone, drawn by websitehq's **Tarot Draw Card**.

**One source of truth (owner, 2026-11-12): the Academy's Tarot series.** The cards, their meanings, the spreads and how a reading is given are the Markdown articles of the Tarot category in the astrohq domain (`rag/consult/tarot-reader/`). The Academy shows them, the guides search them, and this page draws from them (`tarot_deck.py`), so everyone says the same thing about a card. Nothing about a card or a spread is worded in the app.

- **Spreads:** the person picks one: One-Card Draw, Three-Card Spread (the one it starts on), Yes or No, Five-Card Spread. Each is an Academy article (`tarot-spread-<name>`); its places are the numbered list under `## Positions` (`1. **Past** — What led to this situation.`), and its names, hints and how to read it come from there. The longer spreads (Celtic Cross, Forty-Two Card, Thirty-Five Card) are in the Academy for a reader who lays the cards out by hand; to offer one on the page, add its key to `OFFERED` in `tarot_deck.py` (and give the Draw Card a layout for its number of cards).
- **Draw:** anyone asks (an optional question, and a topic) and the cards of the spread come out of the cabinet one after another, all different and drawn from the **whole deck of 78**, each upright or reversed (one in four). Each card's meaning appears below as it lands, with its extra meanings (the minor cards) and a link to its page in the Academy. The draw is chosen in `tarot.py` (`secrets`), kept for a day under a secret token, and the token is put in the sign-in address, so coming back shows the same cards.
- **The reading of the cards together:** written by the hub's text-to-text (the same settings as the daily rashifal: Daily Rashifal Settings) for someone signed in, once per draw, and kept as a **Tarot Reading** (with its `spread`). A visitor sees it locked, with the way to sign in; after signing in they return to the same draw and it is written then. The writer is told: reflection, never a forecast, no medical, legal or money advice (a serious matter: a qualified professional), and to follow the Academy's words on how to read that spread and on giving a reading (the practice notes).
- **Kept readings:** a draw kept before spreads existed holds `{"i", "r"}` (a place among the twenty-two major cards); it still opens, as the three-card spread. The first twenty-two places of the deck are the major cards in order, so that never changes.
- **Where it is shown:** the "Tarot reading" box of the free reports row (vocabulary `reports_soon` with a `link`).

Open: Hindi words for the Academy's text (the page's own words, such as buttons, are translatable; the cards and spreads are English, as the Academy is), the guide that draws and reads cards in chat (a draw tool using `tarot_deck` and `tarot.draw`, and a blueprint), and the cards' own artwork once the owner supplies it.

## Card pictures

**The factory keeps them (owner, 2026-11-13): one copy, for the page, the Academy and the guides' chat.** They are in the agents factory, `domains/astrohq/media/tarot/<card id>.webp`, next to the articles they belong to. AgentsHQ delivers them to the site on every migrate (`raftor_agentshq/media.py`: into `public/files/factory/astrohq/tarot/`) and answers where a picture is; `tarot_deck.image(card id)` asks it, and the page, the Academy's covers (`content._cover`) and the chat tools' cards all use that one answer, so the address is the same everywhere: `/files/factory/astrohq/tarot/<card id>.webp`.

- **Card id:** the name of the card's article without `tarot-card-`: `00-fool` ... `21-world` for the major cards, `<suit>-<rank>` (`wands-ace` ... `pentacles-king`) for the minor cards. `back.webp` is the back of the cards, also the cover of the Tarot pages that are not a card. **Where a picture is not available the cover is left blank (the Academy draws its usual soft blank) and the page draws the card from its symbol: never another series' picture.**
- **Shape:** 1024 x 1536 webp, at most about 150 KB each. Upright pictures: a card that comes out reversed is turned by the page. The card's name is written over the bottom of the picture by the page.
- **Adding or changing one:** put the file in the factory under its id, release the factory, then `bench migrate` on the site (it delivers the files and the Academy's covers pick them up). A card with no picture is drawn from its symbol.
- **Not drawn yet (2026-11-13):** `01-magician`, `wands-seven` and `back`.
- **Fallback:** a site the factory's pictures have not been delivered to is given what an earlier version kept in this app's `public/tarot` (the pictures there were put by hand, 2026-11-13, so the page that was live then shows them). Once the factory's pictures are delivered everywhere, that folder can go.

## Tarot in a chat (the tools)

The page's draw, taken one card at a time, so that a guide can conduct a session. `tarot_session.py` is the engine; `assistant_tools/tarot.py` the three tools (registered in `hooks.py`, read by FAC). The person is never named by the model: the blueprint lists the tools under `guardrails.session_user_tools`, and the server puts the signed-in person into `user`.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `start_tarot_session(spread?, question?, topic?)` | Shuffles the whole deck once (`tarot_deck.shuffled()`, `secrets`) and lays it face down for the person. With no spread, or one the page does not offer, it lists the spreads with what each is for (the Academy's words), to choose from. |
| `deal_tarot_card(pick?, clarifier?, session?)` | Turns over **one** card into the next place. `pick` is a number among the cards still face down (the person chose); left out, the top card is dealt. When the spread is full the answer carries `reading_guide` (the Academy's words on how to read that spread, and the practice notes). `clarifier: true` adds a card after the spread is full, up to two. |
| `get_tarot_session(session?)` | The latest session of the last day: the question, the spread, the cards turned over with what the Academy says of each (what it shows, what it means, upright or reversed, extra meanings, its picture and page), the places still empty. For coming back to the same cards in a later turn. |

- **The guide decides**, from the conversation, whether the person chooses their cards (it asks for a number from 1 to the `face_down` count and passes it as `pick`) or it deals for them. The tool descriptions and the guide's flow say how. Nothing in the engine depends on which.
- **The guide never names or invents a card**: every card it speaks of came out of `deal_tarot_card`. The draw is the server's and is random; the persona boundary "never claim to draw cards unless the product does a documented random draw" is met by this.
- **Kept as a Tarot Reading** with `source` "chat" and the still-face-down deck in `fan` (never given to the guide). A session is open for a day; with no `session` given the person's latest is meant, so the guide carries no token. A new `start_tarot_session` begins a new route; the earlier session is left as it was.
- **Cards under the reply:** the chat already shows cards under a reply, made by code from what the tools returned (the factory's `graph/helpers.py`, drawn by the chat card with the shop's own card). A tarot card is one more kind (`tarot`): the card just dealt is always shown, with its place and way up ("Past · Reversed"), its picture and a "Read more" button to its Academy page; the other cards on the table are shown only when the reply names them, and a card already shown in the conversation is not shown again. The blueprint needs `cards.enabled` (its button word is `cards.read`). The picture is the file named by the card's id (`tarot_deck.image`); until a card has one, the card shows its first letter, as a product without a picture does.
- **The ceremony is the Academy's methodology.** "Conducting a Session" (`tarot-method-conducting-a-session`: opening, choosing the spread, choosing the cards, turning over the cards, giving the reading, going deeper, closing, care) is read by `tarot_deck.conduct()`. `start_tarot_session` returns all of it as `how_to_conduct`; each card dealt, and `get_tarot_session`, return the part needed next. So the guide conducts what the Academy defines (including when the person chooses their cards and when they are dealt), and a change to the article changes the guide with nothing to edit in the guide or in code. The page's writer is given the article's "Care" too.
- **The guide:** the Tarot skill (`ai_skills.json`, area `tarot`), the agent (`agents.TAROT`: user, role and its tools; its Blueprint Reference is made switched off) and the blueprint and personality `tarot` in the factory's astrohq domain are built. It stays **off** until its keys to the tool server are made and a person switches it on: see `docs/ai-guides.md`.

## The deck in code

`tarot_deck.py` reads the articles of the Tarot category through AgentsHQ and keeps the parsed deck for five minutes (`load(force=True)` reads it at once): `cards()` (the order: the major cards by number, then wands, cups, swords and pentacles from the ace to the king), `card(id)`, `spreads()`, `spread(key)`, `practice_notes()` and `image(id)`. What the file holds is not wording: the numeral on a card face, and which symbol of websitehq's icon set stands for a card (one each for the major cards; `wand`, `cup`, `sword`, `coin` for the suits).
