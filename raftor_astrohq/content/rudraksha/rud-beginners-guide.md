## What it is

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | A seed from the Elaeocarpus tree, worn in Shiva devotion |
| What is a mukhi? | The natural faces or divisions on a bead |
| Are meanings guaranteed? | No. They are traditional beliefs |

## How to wear it

| Way | Note |
|---|---|
| Single bead or pendant | Secure thread, no strain on the bead |
| Mala | Check knots and fittings from time to time |
| Daily life | Avoid pulling, impact and harsh chemicals |
| Mantras by place | Ishana, Tatpurusha, Aghora, Vyomavyapi (Rudrakshajabala Upanishad) |
| Rituals | Follow your lineage. No single practice fits everyone |

## How to spot a genuine one

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Natural lines | Grooves from end to end. Scratches are not faces |
| Surface | Texture varies. Perfectly smooth proves nothing |
| Alterations | Carving, filled grooves, joins |
| Photos | Clear views from several angles |
| Papers | Issuer, report number, date, matches this bead |
| Not proof | Floating in water, or a seller's word |

## Care and cleaning

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Wipe dust with a soft dry cloth | Harsh soaps, solvents, chemicals |
| Let it dry naturally if wet | Prolonged soaking |
| Store in a clean dry place | Heat, pressure, household glue |
| Check thread and fittings | Assuming oil is needed |
