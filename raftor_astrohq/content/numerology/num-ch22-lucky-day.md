*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

The simplest rule is to use the recurring dates in a person’s birth-number series. For example, a person born on the 1st treats the 1st, 10th, 19th, and 28th as dates of the same number. The pages on each number layer weekday and period associations onto those dates.

Classical numerology says a date for a request involving another person (such as an employer) is less certain than the recurring personal-date rule. Its worked example is given with the compound numbers. It is its example, not a settled rule, and no basis for any decision.
