*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes 5 as versatile, quick-thinking, impulsive, sociable, excitement-seeking, and risk-tolerant. It portrays it as resilient but liable to nervous exhaustion and irritability.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 5-series dates (5, 14, 23). Wednesday and Friday are favored in this tradition; 5 is said to associate with almost all numbers, most strongly with itself.
- **Colours/jewels:** Light greys, white, glistening materials, and light shades generally; diamond, platinum, silver.
- **Examples named:** St. Louis, Louis XVI, Shakespeare, George VI, the Duke of Windsor, Lord Lister, Karl Marx, Handel, Talleyrand.
