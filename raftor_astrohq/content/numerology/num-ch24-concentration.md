*This is Cheiro's account, from Chapter XXIV of Cheiro's Book of Numbers. It is a historical doctrine, not a fact or a prediction.*

Cheiro advises readers to identify their birth number, study the character he assigns to it, mentally associate themselves with it, and plan decisive actions on dates in its series. Present this as the book’s self-focus exercise, not a verified technique for improving outcomes.
