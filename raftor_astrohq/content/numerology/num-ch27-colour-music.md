*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Tradition refines colour links and makes musical/national-style generalizations. Classical numerology associates 1/3/9 with martial or “full-blooded” music; 2/7 with string and wind instruments; 6 with romantic, sweet, lilting music; 5 with unusual/original music; and 4/8 with organ and choral music described as plaintive, melancholy, religious, or fatalistic. It assigns England/Germany to 9, Ireland to 6, Scotland to 2 and 8, Wales to 4 and 5, and the United States to 5.

These are cultural generalizations, not reliable predictions of an individual’s taste. The rule for primary and secondary colours is in the note on colours and numbers.
