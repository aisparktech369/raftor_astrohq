*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology illustrates assumed sympathy between two people with number 1 and suggests a person 1 meeting a person 2 choose a date in either series and adapt to the other person’s supposed style. It says 1 is associated with 4 but cautions that a 4 should retain an independent viewpoint rather than be dominated by a 1.

These are interaction examples from the tradition, not grounds to profile, manipulate, or judge another person. Use ordinary communication and preparation for real meetings.
