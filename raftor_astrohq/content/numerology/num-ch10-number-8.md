*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology associates 8 with Saturn and presents it as intense, lonely, and often misunderstood. It also describes zeal, determination, public-mindedness, and sympathy for the oppressed, while portraying 8 as a difficult or fatalistic number with extremes of success and failure. The figure is pictured as two equal 4s/two touching circles, linking material and spiritual worlds. Classical numerology distinguishes a revolutionary/upheaval side from a concentrated, religious, philosophical side and invokes reincarnation and divine justice.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 8-series dates (8, 17, 26); Saturday, Sunday, and Monday appear in this tradition. 8 is linked with 4, but later advice varies on whether to avoid or deliberately use these dates.
- **Colours/jewels:** Dark grey, black, dark blue, purple; amethyst, dark sapphire, black pearl, black diamond.
- **Examples named:** Mary I, King Albert, Queen Mary, George Bernard Shaw, David Lloyd George, Rockefeller, Jules Verne, John Wesley.
- **Crippen illustration:** Classical numerology examines repeated 4 and 8 values in Crippen’s life and interprets them as a fadic pattern. This is a retrospective case study, not a method for predicting crime, death, or any user’s fate.
