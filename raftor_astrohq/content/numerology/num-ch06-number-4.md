*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology associates 4 with Uranus and pairs it with 1. It describes number 4 people as unconventional, oppositional, reform-minded, rebellious toward rules, sensitive, melancholy, or misunderstood; classical numerology says material success may be difficult.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 4-series dates (4, 13, 22, 31). Tradition gives Saturday, Sunday, and Monday associations and links 4 variously with 1, 2, 7, and 8.
- **Colours/jewels:** Electric colours, half-shades, electric blue, greys; sapphire.
- **Examples named:** George Washington, Lord Byron, George Eliot, Sarah Bernhardt, Faraday, Francis Bacon, Conan Doyle, Immanuel Kant, Richard Wagner.
- **Note:** classical numerology's advice to people born under 4 changes between the later passages on the numbers 4 and 8 (see Persons Born Under 4 and 8).
