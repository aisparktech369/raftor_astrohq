*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes number 2 as gentle, imaginative, artistic, romantic, and inventive, but less forceful and more mental than number 1. It also attributes restlessness, oversensitivity, despondency, or indecision to some number 2 people. These are historical character claims.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 2-series dates (2, 11, 20, 29). Tradition favors Sunday, Monday, and Friday in its context; it links 2 with 1 and secondarily with 7.
- **Colours/jewels:** Greens, cream, and white; avoid dark colours, especially black, purple, and dark red. Pearls, moonstone, pale-green stones, and jade.
- **Examples named:** Marie Antoinette, Gladstone, Edison, Ibsen, Thomas Hardy, President Harding, Pope Pius X.
