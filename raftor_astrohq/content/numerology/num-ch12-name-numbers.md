*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology says to use the name by which a person is best known. Depending on context, it may use a prominent surname alone, a principal given name, or a professional/social title. A work name and a private or familiar name can differ. It treats the name number as a complement to the birth number.

## Recovered Mystic Alphabet

This is the traditional table:

| Value | Letters |
|---:|---|
| 1 | A, I, J, Q, Y |
| 2 | B, K, R |
| 3 | C, G, L, S |
| 4 | D, M, T |
| 5 | E, H, N, X |
| 6 | U, V, W |
| 7 | O, Z |
| 8 | F, P |
| 9 | No letter assigned |

The traditional table says “I or J = 1.” Classical numerology says 9 is reserved for the “9-lettered name of God”; a name may still total to 9. The table covers Latin letters only. Numerology gives no transliteration rule for other scripts or diacritics; for another script, choose the Latin spelling you want counted.

## Name calculation procedure

1. Choose the spelling and context to count: familiar or private, professional, public, or title-bearing.
2. Map each A–Z letter using the table above. Spaces and punctuation have no value.
3. Add the letters in each word, and keep each word's compound beside its root number.
4. Where the roots of the words are combined, that is a step of its own. The examples do not all combine them in exactly the same way.
5. Show every addition, and use only this alphabet.

**Worked source example:** JOHN = J(1)+O(7)+H(5)+N(5)=18→9. SMITH = S(3)+M(4)+I(1)+T(4)+H(5)=17→8. Classical numerology then combines the roots: 9+8=17→8. Both word compounds (18 and 17) and the full-name compound and root (17→8) are kept.

## Selected name interpretations

- **Lloyd George:** word roots 9 and 7 combine to 16, “Shattered Citadel”; David Lloyd George is also discussed as a different commonly used name.
- **Baldwin:** 22→4, interpreted as a good person vulnerable to others’ folly.
- **Austen Chamberlain:** word roots combine to 17; titles and common usage alter the reading.
- **Ramsay MacDonald:** classical numerology combines name parts and interprets 12, 32, 8, and 44 as “victim/sacrifice,” “paths of wisdom,” and a warning pattern.

These are readings of selected public figures, not independently verified outcomes.
