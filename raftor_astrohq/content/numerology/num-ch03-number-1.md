*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes number 1 as creative, inventive, individual, ambitious, authoritative, determined, and sometimes obstinate. It says number 1 people dislike restraint and seek to direct their own lives. It associates them with the Sun.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 1-series dates (1, 10, 19, 28); classical numerology discusses Sunday and Monday and favorable periods. It says 1 gets on with 2, 4, and 7.
- **Colours/jewels:** Gold, yellow, bronze, golden-brown; topaz, amber, yellow diamond.
- **Examples named:** Alexander the Great, the Duke of Wellington, Annie Besant, Woodrow Wilson, Herbert Hoover, Orville Wright, Chopin, Bismarck, Goethe, Edgar Allan Poe.
