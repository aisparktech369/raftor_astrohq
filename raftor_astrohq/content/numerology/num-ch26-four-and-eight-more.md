*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

The guidance on 4 and 8 varies from one passage to another. Here are the branches, side by side:

- Classical numerology often says people born under 4 or 8 should avoid intensifying those numbers and may use name numbers such as 1, 3, 5, or 6.
- It says 4-series people may use the associated 1 series and sometimes 2/7 dates as interchangeable or acceptable.
- If 4/8 combinations recur with adverse events, it says to avoid both series; in some cases it suggests the Zodiac-period number instead.
- For December 21–January 20 (number 8 positive), it suggests the opposite period’s 2–7 series. For January 21–February 19 (number 8 negative), it states the opposite July period is 1–4. This conflicts with blanket advice to avoid 4 and 8 because 1–4 includes 4.
- It also says a number 8 person may choose to use the full 8 force and 4/8 dates, accepting a more “fatalistic” life.

This doctrine varies from one passage to another. None of these branches is advice for any person.
