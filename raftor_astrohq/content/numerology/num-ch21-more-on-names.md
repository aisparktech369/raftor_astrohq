*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology contrasts the Chaldean/Hebrew-style alphabet used in this tradition with a Pythagorean table. Our calculations use the traditional table for name numbers, never the Pythagorean one.

Tradition discusses how names and titles used publicly, professionally, or privately affect the interpretation. Examples include Napoleon and Nellie Melba; classical numerology says the stage/signature name “Nellie Melba” totals 10→1. These are case studies. It counts the spelling the person was known by in that context, which may differ from a legal name.

Tradition also responds to colour questions and supplements number-colour associations. The note on colours gives the clearest hierarchy of primary birth-day colour and secondary period colour.
