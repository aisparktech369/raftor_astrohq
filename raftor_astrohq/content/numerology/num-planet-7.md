*Numbers are linked to planets in numerology, and the tradition links each planet with a gem, a Rudraksha bead and a pooja. This page joins the two. It is what the tradition says, not a promise of any result.*

## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Number | 7 (days of the month 7, 16, 25) |
| Planet in Indian numerology (Ank Jyotish) | Ketu |
| Planet in Cheiro's book | Neptune |
| Gem the tradition links with Ketu | Cat's eye (Lehsunia) |
| Rudraksha | 9 mukhi |
| Day | Tuesday or Thursday |
| Deity | Ganesha |
| Pooja | Ketu graha shanti pooja |

## How the number and the planet are joined

Cheiro's book gives the number 7 to Neptune, which Jyotish does not use, so the remedies on this page follow the Indian planet Ketu. A qualified astrologer judges the birth chart first and says which planet matters for you, because a planet that helps in one chart may not in another. The number is a pointer, not a prescription.
