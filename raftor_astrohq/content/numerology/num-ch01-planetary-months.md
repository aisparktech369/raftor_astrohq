*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology maps numbers to planets, signs, weekday groups, and positive/negative aspects. The weekday sequence is Sunday 1–4; Monday 2–7; Tuesday 9; Wednesday 5; Thursday 3; Friday 6; Saturday 8. It treats 1–4 and 2–7 as related pairs; 3–6–9 are sympathetic/interchangeable in some contexts, not as one universal compatibility chart.

| Sign | Dates | Number | Ruler/aspect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Aries | March 21–April 19 | 9 | Mars, positive |
| Taurus | April 20–May 20 | 6 | Venus, positive |
| Gemini | May 21–June 20 | 5 | Mercury, positive |
| Cancer | June 21–July 20 | 2–7 | Moon, positive |
| Leo | July 21–August 20 | 1–4 | Sun, positive |
| Virgo | August 21–September 20 | 5 | Mercury, negative |
| Libra | September 21–October 20 | 6 | Venus, negative |
| Scorpio | October 21–November 20 | 9 | Mars, negative |
| Sagittarius | November 21–December 20 | 3 | Jupiter, positive |
| Capricorn | December 21–January 20 | 8 | Saturn, positive |
| Aquarius | January 21–February 21 | 8 | Saturn, negative |
| Pisces | February 19–March 20 | 3 | Jupiter, negative |

Classical numerology describes a positive sign as more physical/forceful and a negative sign as more mental. It says a seven-day cusp at the beginning and end weakens the number and blends qualities of adjoining signs. Later passages use slightly different cusp endpoints. The date ranges overlap at the Aquarius/Pisces boundary. The traditional text is inconsistent about the number of signs, and we count twelve.

Classical numerology illustrates positive 9/Aries as a physical warrior and negative 9/Scorpio as a mentally oriented warrior. It also gives elemental triangles: Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn). These are source classifications, not additional calculations.
