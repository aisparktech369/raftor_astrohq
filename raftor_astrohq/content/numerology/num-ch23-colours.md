*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology clarifies that the birth-day number’s colours are primary; the Zodiac/month-period colours are secondary. Example: a person born on a 6 date during a 9 period should use 6 colours primarily and 9 colours secondarily. Classical numerology says blue may cross from the opposing Venus/6 sign in a negative 9 period. Later colour details include full blue rather than electric blue for 6 and special date exceptions for red/pink.

Colour and jewel recommendations are historical symbolic advice. They are not protection, treatment or a guaranteed effect.
