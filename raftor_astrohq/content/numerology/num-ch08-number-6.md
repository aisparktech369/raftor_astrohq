*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes 6 as magnetic, determined, loving, romantic or idealistic, artistic, generous, and hospitable. It says number 6 dislikes discord and jealousy but may fight fiercely for loved ones or causes.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 6-series dates (6, 15, 24). Tradition favors Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday and associates 6 strongly with 3 and 9.
- **Colours/jewels:** Blues; rose/pink as secondary; avoid black and dark purple. Turquoise and emerald.
- **Examples named:** Queen Victoria, Napoleon I, Frederick the Great, Oliver Cromwell, Joan of Arc, Cecil Rhodes, Susan B. Anthony, Alfred Tennyson.
