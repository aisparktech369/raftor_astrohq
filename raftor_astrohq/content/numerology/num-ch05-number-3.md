*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes number 3 as ambitious, orderly, disciplined, and conscientious, with an inclination toward authority. It also warns of pride, dictatorial behavior, independence, and resistance to restraint.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 3-series dates (3, 12, 21, 30). Tradition favors Thursday, Friday, and Tuesday and calls 3 sympathetic with 6 and 9.
- **Colours/jewels:** Mauve, violet, and purple; blue, crimson, and rose as secondary colours; amethyst.
- **Examples named:** George V, Abraham Lincoln, Winston Churchill, Rudyard Kipling, Darwin, Mark Twain, Voltaire, Ramsay MacDonald.
