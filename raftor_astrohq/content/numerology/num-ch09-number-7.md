*This is classical numerology, a historical doctrine. It is not a fact or a prediction.*

Classical numerology describes 7 as independent, original, change- and travel-loving, philosophical, and less materialistic. It associates it with unusual religious interests, dreams, intuition, occult matters, and a quieting magnetism.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 7-series dates (7, 16, 25). Tradition favors Sunday and Monday and links 7 with 2; some passages use 1, 2, and 4 as interchangeable dates.
- **Colours/jewels:** Pale greens, white, and yellow; avoid heavy dark colours. Moonstone, cat’s-eye, pearls, moss agate.
- **Examples named:** Queen Elizabeth, Louis XIV, Charles Dickens, Oscar Wilde, Isaac Newton, Andrew Carnegie, William Wordsworth.
