## How to read this series

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Whose numerology is this? | Cheiro's, from his Book of Numbers (the Chaldean style): the birth number comes from the day of the month alone, and names use his own letter table where nothing is valued 9 |
| Is it a fact or a prediction? | Neither. It is a historical belief system. Say "Cheiro says", never "you will" |
| Who does the sums? | The calculator, by fixed rules, with every addition shown. Never guess a number by eye |
| Is any other numerology mixed in? | No. Pythagorean life-path numbers and Indian Ank Jyotish are different systems and give different answers for the same name |

## What it is not for

| Subject | Our rule |
|---|---|
| Health, medicine, herbs | Never. A number does not diagnose or treat. See a qualified doctor |
| Death, injury, crime, violence | Never predicted for anyone. Cheiro's grim readings (for example some compound numbers) are described only as his symbols |
| Money, investments, betting | Never advice. Cheiro's racing scheme is history, not a method |
| Law and contracts | Never advice |
| Relationships | A number does not make a person faithful, false, compatible or dangerous |
| Changing a legal name, moving or changing a job | Never advised because of a number |

| Question | Answer |
