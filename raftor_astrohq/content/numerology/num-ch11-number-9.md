*This is Cheiro's account, from Chapter XI of Cheiro's Book of Numbers. It is a historical doctrine, not a fact or a prediction.*

Cheiro associates 9 with Mars, energy, force, war, destruction, leadership, and finality. He describes number 9 as courageous, ambitious, impulsive, independent, and forceful, while warning of enemies, accidents, violence, fire, and injury.

- **Date and affinity claims:** Use 9-series dates (9, 18, 27). Tuesday is emphasized; Thursday and Friday also appear. The chapter calls 9 sympathetic with 3 and 6.
- **Colours/jewels:** Crimson/red, rose, pink; ruby, garnet, bloodstone.
- **Examples named:** Kaiser Wilhelm, Edward VII, Theodore Roosevelt, Grover Cleveland, Ulysses Grant, Paganini, George Stephenson.
- **Symbolism:** Cheiro says multiplication by 9 returns to 9 under digit addition; he interprets 666→18→9 as “the number of man” and contrasts 888→24→6/Love. These are his religious-number readings.
