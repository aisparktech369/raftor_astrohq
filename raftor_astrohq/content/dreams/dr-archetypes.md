*These readings are the Institute's reflective notes, written as working interpretations. They are ideas to try on, not a code: your own feeling about an image always comes first.*

## The Shadow

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Hidden or rejected parts of the personality. |
| Images often linked with it | dark figures, monsters, dangerous animals, being chased |
| One possibility | The dream may reflect a part of the self that is asking to be acknowledged. |

## The Hero Journey

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Overcoming obstacles and growing through struggle. |
| Images often linked with it | climbing mountains, battle, travel through difficult terrain |
| One possibility | The dream may reflect a challenge that leads to personal growth. |

## The Wise Guide

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Inner wisdom appearing in symbolic form. |
| Images often linked with it | guru, teacher, elder, sage |
| One possibility | The dream may suggest guidance emerging from the subconscious. |


## How to use them

An archetype is a metaphor you can try on. A dream does not prove one, and it does not show a trauma, a repressed memory or a condition.
