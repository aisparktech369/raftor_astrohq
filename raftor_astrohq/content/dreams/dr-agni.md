*These are compiled notes about a traditional source. The exact passage, edition and translation have not yet been checked against the original, so they are not given as scripture. They describe what the tradition is said to hold; they do not predict anything about you.*

## How the tradition sorts dreams

The notes sort dreams as auspicious or inauspicious omens, and say their effects depend on when the dream comes and what is seen in it. This is how the tradition reads omens. It is not how a dream is read for you here.

## Dreams the tradition reads as favourable

| Dream | How the tradition reads it |
|---|---|
| Climbing a hill or mansion | Progress or success |
| Riding an elephant, horse, or bull | Prosperity and power |
| Seeing white flowers | Purity and good fortune |
| Wearing white garments | Peace and auspicious outcomes |
| Washing oneself | Purification and renewal |
| Raising a banner or flag | Victory or recognition |
| Victory over enemies | Success in conflict |
| Success in disputes or battles | Triumph and achievement |
| Clear sky | Clarity and positive developments |
| Drinking milk | Nourishment and prosperity |
| Favor from gods or teachers | Blessings and guidance |
| Falling from the horns of a cow | Good fortune |
| Becoming a ruler or king | Elevation in status |
| Seeing gold | Wealth and prosperity |
| Seeing cows or bulls | Abundance and strength |
| Trees filled with fruits | Growth and prosperity |
| Clear water | Purity and emotional harmony |

## Dreams the tradition reads as unfavourable

These are listed because they are part of the source. A frightening image in a dream is not a sign that the reading will come true.

| Dream | How the tradition reads it |
|---|---|
| Grass or trees growing on the body | Disorder or imbalance |
| Breaking bronze on the head | Possible misfortune |
| Shaving of the head | Loss or sorrow |
| Remaining nude | Vulnerability or humiliation |
| Wearing torn clothes | Poverty or distress |
| Anointing the body with oil | Illness or weakness |
| Being covered with mud | Humiliation or hardship |
| Falling from a great height | Loss of position or security |
| Riding animals such as pig, dog, ass, or camel | Degradation or misfortune |
| Seeing red flowers and trees | Danger or conflict |
| Drowning in a river | Emotional distress or danger |
| Bathing in muddy or impure water | Impurity or confusion |
| Being afflicted by disease | Illness or suffering |
| Buildings collapsing | Instability or loss |
| Playing with goblins or spirits | Association with harmful influences |
| Being insulted by others | Humiliation or conflict |
| Wearing ochre robes while playing | Loss of dignity or social disruption |
| Immersion in oil or intoxicating drinks | Moral or physical decline |

## Remedies the tradition describes for bad dreams

The notes say the texts suggest rituals to neutralise a bad dream: bathing, worshipping Brahmins, oblations with sesame seeds, worship of deities such as Vishnu, Brahma, Shiva, Surya and Ganesha, and reciting hymns such as the Purusha Sukta. These are described as practices of the tradition. None is presented as necessary, and none as a guaranteed result.

## Timing notes in the Agni Purana

| When the dream comes | What the note says |
|---|---|
| First quarter of the night | The result may come within one year |
| Second quarter | Within six months |
| Third quarter | Within three months |
| Fourth quarter | Within fifteen days |
| At sunrise | Within ten days |
| Several dreams in one night | The later dream is counted as more significant |

These timing notes are described as the Agni Purana's. A different set of timing notes is on its own page and the two are not the same.
