*These readings are the Institute's reflective notes, written as working interpretations. They are ideas to try on, not a code: your own feeling about an image always comes first.*


## Actions: Flying to Jumping

| Symbol | What it often stands for | One possibility |
|---|---|---|
| Flying | Freedom, ambition, transcendence | May represent overcoming limitations or gaining perspective. |
| Falling | Insecurity, loss of control | May reflect anxiety about a situation in waking life. |
| Running | Urgency or avoidance | May indicate trying to escape a problem. |
| Being Chased | Fear, unresolved conflict | May represent avoiding something important. |
| Hiding | Fear of confrontation | May indicate reluctance to face a situation. |
| Fighting | Internal or external conflict | May represent emotional struggle. |
| Winning a Fight | Confidence, empowerment | May symbolize overcoming challenges. |
| Losing a Fight | Vulnerability | May reflect feelings of defeat. |
| Climbing | Progress, effort | May represent striving toward goals. |
| Jumping | Risk or decision | May symbolize taking a leap of faith. |
