## The DREAM steps

| Step | Name | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| D | Describe | You tell the dream in your own words, in order, with the phrases that stayed with you. Fragments are fine. A list of symbols does not come first |
| R | Read the story | The setting, the characters, what happens, the conflict, the turning point, the ending and what changed are picked out |
| E | Explore feeling and association | What felt strongest, and what the main image means to you. A link to waking life is made only if you want it |
| A | Apply a lens | One lens is chosen: reflective, archetypal, or a named traditional source. Each is labelled |
| M | Make a tentative synthesis | A small number of possibilities, each tied to details of the dream, one question to explore, and what cannot be concluded |

## Reading a dream as a story

| Part of the story | What to notice |
|---|---|
| Setting | Where it happens. A forest may feel like uncertainty or exploration, a house like personal identity, a temple like reflection, a city like social pressure, an ocean like deep feeling |
| Characters | Who is there, and who the dreamer is in the scene |
| Conflict | What is in the way, or what is feared or wanted |
| Turning point | The moment something changes |
| Ending | How it resolves, and what changed for the dreamer |

## What gets the most attention

An editorial rule of thumb, not a scientific score: a prominent, repeated, unusual or emotionally intense image may deserve more attention than a background one. The dreamer's own association always outranks it.

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| Primary | A large snake appearing suddenly, a burning house, a divine figure |
| Secondary | Trees, background objects, minor characters |
| Order of attention | Emotionally powerful images, then unusual or vivid ones, then repeated ones, then the surroundings |

The same image can weigh differently with different feelings: a snake met with fear carries more weight than a snake met with mild curiosity, and the two are read differently.

## Questions about your life, only if you wish

| Question |
|---|
| Is anything changing in your life recently? |
| Has something been causing you stress or excitement? |
| Does the dream remind you of a current situation? |

Life areas often looked at are career, relationships, health, personal goals and spiritual growth. Linking an image to your own life is your choice, and you can decline.
