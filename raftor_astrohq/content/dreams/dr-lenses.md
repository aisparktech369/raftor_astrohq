## The lenses

| Lens | What it does | What it rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Personal and reflective | Starts from your own meaning for an image and offers a possible metaphor afterwards: "Does that fit for you?" | Your associations |
| Narrative | Reads the dream as a story with a setting, characters, conflict, turning point and ending. It is a way of looking, not a diagnosis | The story itself |
| Archetypal | Offers the Shadow, the Hero's Journey and the Wise Guide as optional metaphors. It does not claim a dream proves an archetype, a trauma or a condition | The Institute's reflective notes |
| Indian traditional | Uses only the notes of the source you choose: Agni Purana omens, Swapna Shastra notes, Ayurvedic notes or timing notes. The sources are kept apart and never merged into one Indian rule | Compiled notes, labelled |
| Astrology | A separate overlay that would use your birth details, and only with your consent. The notes here on lunar day, weekday and timing are not a chart reading | Not part of these pages |

## How well is a source known

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified from a primary text | The exact edition, translation and passage have been checked. No note on these pages has reached this yet |
| Cited from a named author | A named author or publication is given, and it is credited as that author's view, not as the original tradition |
| Institute editorial | The Institute's own working interpretation, an idea and not a source fact |
| Named tradition, passage not yet verified | A tradition is named but the exact passage is missing. It is not given as scripture, and this is said each time |

## Why the sources are kept apart

The same symbol can have different readings in different sources, and timing rules differ too. Each reading stays with its source, so you can see where it comes from and choose which to use.
