*These readings are the Institute's reflective notes, written as working interpretations. They are ideas to try on, not a code: your own feeling about an image always comes first.*


## Places: House to Cemetery

| Symbol | What it often stands for | One possibility |
|---|---|---|
| House | The self, personal identity | Different rooms may represent different aspects of personality. |
| Childhood Home | Memories, past influences | May represent unresolved emotions from earlier life. |
| Unknown House | Unexplored aspects of the mind | May symbolize discovering hidden abilities. |
| Temple | Spirituality, guidance | May indicate a search for meaning or inner peace. |
| Church or Sacred Place | Faith, moral reflection | May symbolize spiritual questioning. |
| School | Learning, growth | May reflect life lessons or self-improvement. |
| Classroom | Intellectual development | May symbolize gaining knowledge. |
| Library | Wisdom, accumulated knowledge | May represent searching for answers. |
| Hospital | Healing, recovery | May indicate emotional or physical healing. |
| Cemetery | Endings, reflection | May represent letting go of the past. |
