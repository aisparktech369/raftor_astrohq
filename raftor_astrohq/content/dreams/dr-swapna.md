*These are compiled notes about a traditional source. The exact passage, edition and translation have not yet been checked against the original, so they are not given as scripture. They describe what the tradition is said to hold; they do not predict anything about you.*

Swapna Shastra is a broad name for traditional Indian dream interpretation, and no single text is named for these notes. The notes say meaning depends on context, feeling and timing.
