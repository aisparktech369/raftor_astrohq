*These readings are the Institute's reflective notes, written as working interpretations. They are ideas to try on, not a code: your own feeling about an image always comes first.*


## Feelings: Fear to Compassion

| Symbol | What it often stands for | One possibility |
|---|---|---|
| Fear | Anxiety, insecurity | May indicate unresolved stress or perceived threats. |
| Panic | Overwhelming stress | May reflect loss of control in waking life. |
| Anxiety | Uncertainty or worry | May symbolize concerns about future events. |
| Confusion | Lack of clarity | May represent difficulty making decisions. |
| Curiosity | Exploration | May indicate openness to new experiences. |
| Joy | Fulfillment or satisfaction | May represent harmony or personal success. |
| Excitement | Anticipation | May reflect optimism about upcoming opportunities. |
| Relief | Release from tension | May symbolize resolution of problems. |
| Love | Emotional connection | May represent meaningful relationships. |
| Compassion | Empathy and care | May indicate emotional maturity. |
