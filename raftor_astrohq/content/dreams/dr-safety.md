## The short version

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can a dream predict what will happen? | No dream symbol and no timing rule can show that a future event will happen. The traditions describe omens; they do not give certainty |
| Is there one meaning for each symbol? | No. A snake, a house or a river can mean different things to different dreamers, and the pages give possibilities, not rules |
| Whose meaning comes first? | The dreamer's own. If your meaning differs from a catalogue, yours is the one that counts |
| Is this a therapy or a medical service? | No. It is a reflective and traditional reading, not therapy, medicine or astrology of your chart |

## What a dream cannot show

| A dream cannot show | Why |
|---|---|
| A future illness, a pregnancy or a mental-health condition | Dream images do not diagnose anything |
| That someone is false, unfaithful or dangerous | A person in a dream is a part of your own story, not evidence about them |
| A death or a loss that will come | A dream of a relative dying does not establish that it will happen; dreams often carry feelings in strong pictures |
| That a ritual is necessary | A ritual described in a tradition is described as a practice, never as something you must do |

## If a dream frightens you

Dreams often exaggerate feelings and rarely point to a real danger. Fear in a dream usually reflects feelings we may already carry. If bad dreams come often and disturb your sleep or your days, speaking with a doctor or another qualified health professional is the right step. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis line at once.

## Your dreams and your privacy

| Topic | How it works |
|---|---|
| Is a dream kept? | Only if you choose it. Remembering dreams from one conversation to the next is off unless you turn it on |
| What can be kept | You are told what would be saved: the dream itself, a summary or tags |
| Control | You can look at what is kept, correct it, export it and delete it, and leave any dream out of pattern tracking |
| Separation | Dream text is kept apart from account details and from any birth details |
| Use | Your dreams are not used to train anything without a separate, explicit yes |

## Where the research stands

Modern dream research still has open questions. Readings on these pages are reflective or taken from tradition. They are not universal decoding rules.
