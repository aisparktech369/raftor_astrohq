## What can be followed

| Kind of pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Recurring images | The same symbol appearing again |
| Recurring feelings | The same strong feeling across dreams |
| Recurring scenes | The same scenario, such as being chased |
| A story that changes | Dream 1: being chased. Dream 2: turning to face the pursuer. Dream 3: the chase is over. This may reflect growing confidence |
| A feeling that changes | Fear, then curiosity, then calm. This may reflect a feeling being worked through |

## Only with your yes

Following dreams across conversations needs your explicit choice. It is off unless you turn it on, you are told what is kept, and you can look at it, correct it, export it, delete it, or leave any dream out. Without it, no pattern across earlier conversations is claimed.

## What a pattern does and does not mean

A pattern says that something came back. It does not say why, and it does not say what will happen. It can be a prompt for reflection or a page in a journal, nothing more.
