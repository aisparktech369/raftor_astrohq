*These are compiled notes about a traditional source. The exact passage, edition and translation have not yet been checked against the original, so they are not given as scripture. They describe what the tradition is said to hold; they do not predict anything about you.*

The notes say that in Swapna Shastra and Jyotisha the timing of a dream is thought to matter, and that timing does not guarantee future events. It is a supporting factor, never the only basis for a reading.

## Time of night

| When | What the note says |
|---|---|
| Early night | Usually influenced by the day's experiences, with little predictive significance |
| Middle of the night | May reflect subconscious thoughts or emotions; symbolic but not usually predictive |
| Late night | Considered more meaningful; deeper impressions |
| Pre-dawn, just before sunrise | Traditional belief holds these dreams most likely to correspond to real events |

## How soon a dream may show itself (this source)

| When | What the note says |
|---|---|
| Early in the night | Possible manifestation after a long period |
| Middle of the night | After several months |
| Late at night | Within weeks |
| Before sunrise | Within days |

These are called symbolic and not deterministic. They differ from the Agni Purana's timing notes on its own page, and the two sets are not combined.

## The lunar day (tithi)

| Tithi | What the note says |
|---|---|
| Ekadashi | Associated with spiritual reflection; dreams may relate to inner growth |
| Purnima (full moon) | Dreams may be vivid and emotionally intense |
| Amavasya (new moon) | Dreams may relate to subconscious fears or hidden thoughts |

## The weekday

| Day | Dreams said to relate to |
|---|---|
| Sunday | Authority, reputation or leadership |
| Monday | Emotions and family |
| Tuesday | Conflict or courage |
| Wednesday | Communication and learning |
| Thursday | Wisdom or spiritual guidance |
| Friday | Relationships and beauty |
| Saturday | Responsibility or karma |

## Using the notes

Timing is one factor beside the symbols, the feeling and your life. It is not a chart reading, and it does not need your birth details.
