*These readings are the Institute's reflective notes, written as working interpretations. They are ideas to try on, not a code: your own feeling about an image always comes first.*


## Animals

| Symbol | What it often stands for | One possibility |
|---|---|---|
| Elephant | Power, wisdom, authority | May indicate leadership, success, or growing influence. |
| Lion | Courage, strength, dominance | May reflect inner power or confrontation with authority. |
| Tiger | Raw power, instinct | May represent intense emotions or strong challenges. |
| Snake | Transformation, hidden fears, renewal | May indicate inner change or subconscious tension. |
| Cow | Nourishment, prosperity, protection | May represent abundance or emotional support. |
| Horse | Freedom, movement, vitality | May symbolize personal drive or ambition. |
| Dog | Loyalty, protection | May represent friendship or trust. |
| Cat | Intuition, mystery | May symbolize independence or hidden insight. |
| Bird | Freedom, spiritual perspective | May represent rising above challenges. |
| Owl | Wisdom, hidden knowledge | May indicate intuition or learning. |
