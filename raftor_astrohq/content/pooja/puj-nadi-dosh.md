## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | The same Nadi (Adi, Madhya or Antya) for the boy and the girl, which scores zero of eight |
| How it is read | The Nadi koota of the Ashtakoota match |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The match | The Nadi koota is the largest, eight of thirty-six points. It stands for health and the line of the family in the tradition |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When | Before marriage, when the match shows the dosha |
| Cancellation | Many astrologers say it is cancelled in some cases, such as the same nakshatra but a different quarter |
| Day | A day the priest advises |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | Both names and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Maha Mrityunjaya jaap | The Shiva mantra is chanted |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Daan | A gift of grain or gold, as advised |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Maha Mrityunjaya mantra | The Rigveda mantra to Shiva |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Should we call off the marriage?</summary><p>That is for the families and <a href="/consult">an astrologer</a> to decide. Many couples with this dosha marry, because cancelling rules apply or the other points are strong.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is there a medical meaning?</summary><p>Some say it is about the line of descendants, with no medical basis. Ask your doctor about any health concern.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
