## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Birth in Ashwini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula or Revati |
| How it is read | The Moon's nakshatra at birth |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The six nakshatras | They fall at the end or start of a sign, and are ruled by Ketu and Mercury. The tradition treats them as junctions |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | The twenty-seventh day after birth, when the same nakshatra returns |
| Who | The parents and the child |
| Opinion | Practices differ on which quarter of the nakshatra matters |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash with water from many sources | For the bath |
| Grains, flowers, rice | Offered |
| Havan items | For the fire |
| Clothes, gifts | Given to priests |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The parents' names and the birth star are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Nakshatra pooja | The deity of the nakshatra is invited |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Abhishek | The child is bathed with the blessed water |
| Daan and prasad | A gift is made and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namah Shivaya | A common mantra |
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is a Gand mool baby unlucky?</summary><p>No. It is a junction, and the tradition asks for a pooja to mark it. Many such children thrive.</p></details>

<details><summary>What if the day has passed?</summary><p>Ask the priest. The pooja can be done on the next return of the nakshatra.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
