## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Moon (Chandra) |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Moon, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Monday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Chandra and the churning of the ocean | In the Puranas the Moon rose from the Samudra Manthan, the churning of the ocean by gods and demons. Shiva wears its crescent on his head (Chandrashekhara) |
| The Moon's phases | In the Puranas the Moon grows and wanes because of the curse of Daksha, and Shiva carries the crescent |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Rice | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of white colour | Offered or donated |
| White flowers, milk, white sweets | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Moon is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Monday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Shram Shrim Shraum Sah Chandramase Namah | The beej mantra of Moon |
| Chandra Stotra | A hymn recited with it |
