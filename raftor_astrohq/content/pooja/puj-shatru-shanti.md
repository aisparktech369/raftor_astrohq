## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Durga, Hanuman, or the family deity |
| What | A pooja with Durga Saptashati verses or the Hanuman Chalisa |
| When | Tuesdays or Navratri |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for trouble from enemies |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Durga | The Devi Mahatmya tells how the Goddess defeated the demons who threatened the gods |
| Hanuman | Hanuman is prayed to for courage and protection |
| The meaning | The prayer is for protection and for a clear mind, and not for harm to another |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday, or Navratri |
| Time | Morning after a bath |
| A caution | Ill will to another harms the one who holds it. Good priests decline requests to harm |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| An image of Durga or Hanuman | The seat of the pooja |
| Red flowers, red cloth | Offered to Durga |
| Sindoor, jasmine oil | Offered to Hanuman |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Durga and Hanuman pooja | They are offered the upachara |
| Recitation | Verses of the Devi Mahatmya or the Chalisa are recited |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Dum Durgayai Namah | For Durga |
| Om Hanumate Namah | For Hanuman |
