## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Baglamukhi, also called Pitambara |
| What | A pooja and japa in yellow |
| When | Tuesdays, Navratri or Baglamukhi Jayanti |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for a court case |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The Mahavidyas | Baglamukhi is one of the ten Mahavidyas, the great forms of the Goddess |
| Her form | She is shown in yellow, holding the tongue of a demon, as a sign of stilling harmful speech |
| Her temples | Datia in Madhya Pradesh and Nalkheda are known seats of her worship |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday, Navratri or Baglamukhi Jayanti (Vaishakha) |
| Time | A priest chooses it from the panchang |
| A caution | The pooja is for protection. The texts warn against using it to harm anyone |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Yellow cloth and flowers | Her colour is yellow |
| Turmeric, yellow rice | Offered |
| Chana dal laddu, banana | The food offered |
| Incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Baglamukhi pooja | She is invited and offered the upachara in yellow |
| Japa | Her mantra is chanted |
| Havan | An offering into the fire, if a havan is done |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Hleem Baglamukhyai Namah | The beej mantra of Baglamukhi |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Will it win my case?</summary><p>No. It is a prayer for protection. A case is decided by the facts and the court, and you need a lawyer.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it meant to harm an enemy?</summary><p>No. It asks for protection and steadiness.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No. It is an act of devotion and not a guaranteed outcome.</p></details>
