## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, Lakshmi and Kubera |
| What | A pooja and havan for a new shop, office or workplace |
| When | On a muhurat from the panchang, before the first sale |
| Who | The owner, with a priest |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Ganesha first | Every beginning starts with Ganesha |
| Lakshmi and Kubera | Lakshmi is honoured for prosperity and Kubera as the keeper of wealth |
| The books | Traders worship their new account books on the Diwali evening (Chopda pujan) |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | A muhurat from the panchang for the owner and the business |
| Time | Before opening to customers |
| Place | The shop or office itself |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash, coconut, mango leaves | The seat of the divine |
| Images of Ganesha and Lakshmi | The seat of the pooja |
| Rice, roli, flowers, turmeric | For the offerings |
| Coins or notes, a new account book | Kept before the deity |
| Sweets, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The owner's name and the business are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Kalash and Lakshmi | Lakshmi and Kubera are invited and offered the upachara |
| The threshold | A lemon-and-chilli string or a toran is hung, as the custom is |
| Aarti | The lamp is waved |
| First sale | The first sale is made after the pooja, and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |
| Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah | For Lakshmi |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Can I do it myself?</summary><p>Yes. Many owners do a short pooja themselves. A priest is asked for the full rite.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee the business will do well?</summary><p>No. A business is built on service and care. The pooja is a blessing at the start.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
