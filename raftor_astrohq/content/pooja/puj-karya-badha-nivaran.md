## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, with Hanuman or Surya for strength and standing |
| What | A pooja with the Ganesh Atharvashirsha and mantras |
| When | Wednesdays, Chaturthi or a day from the panchang |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for job and promotion obstacles |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Vighnaharta | Ganesha is the remover of obstacles |
| Surya | The Sun stands for authority and standing, and is prayed to by those seeking recognition |
| The meaning | The pooja is a prayer for steadiness while one works. It does not change a decision |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Wednesday for Ganesha, Sunday for Surya |
| Time | Morning after a bath |
| Alongside | Work on the skills, the file and the people involved |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Image of Ganesha | The seat of the pooja |
| Durva, red flowers, sindoor | Offered to Ganesha |
| Water and red flowers for Surya | Offered at sunrise if done |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is invited and offered the upachara |
| Atharvashirsha | The Upanishad is recited |
| Japa | Ganesh mantras are chanted |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |
| Om Suryaya Namah | For Surya |
