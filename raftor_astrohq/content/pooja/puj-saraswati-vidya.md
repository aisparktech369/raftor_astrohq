## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Saraswati |
| What | A pooja, with books and pens before the goddess |
| When | Vasant Panchami, Wednesdays or before an exam |
| Who | The student, or the family |
| Time taken | About an hour |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for education |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Saraswati | The Rigveda praises Saraswati as a river and as the goddess of speech and learning |
| Vasant Panchami | On the fifth day of the bright half of Magha, Saraswati is worshipped, and children are often begun in letters (Vidyarambh) |
| The books | Books, pens and instruments are placed before her, and not used that day |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Vasant Panchami, or before an exam or the start of a course |
| Time | Morning after a bath |
| The prayer | For clear thought and steady study |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| An image of Saraswati | The seat of the pooja |
| White flowers, white sweets | Her colour is white |
| Books, pens, instruments | Placed before her |
| Incense, lamp, yellow cloth | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The student's name and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Saraswati pooja | She is invited and offered the upachara |
| Vandana | The Saraswati prayer is recited |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Aim Saraswatyai Namah | A common mantra for Saraswati |
| Ya Kundendu-tushara-hara-dhavala | The first line of the Saraswati prayer |
