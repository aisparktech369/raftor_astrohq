## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Agni, with Ganesha, Lakshmi and Kubera |
| What | A havan with the Shri Suktam and Ganesh and Lakshmi mantras |
| When | Fridays, or a day from the panchang |
| Who | The owner, with a priest |
| Time taken | Two to three hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for business loss |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Agni | Agni carries the offering to the gods |
| Ganesha and Lakshmi | Ganesha clears the way and Lakshmi gives steady means |
| The meaning | The havan is a prayer at a hard time. It does not change the books |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Friday, or a muhurat from the panchang |
| Place | The shop or office, or the home |
| Alongside | A review of costs, customers and credit with someone who knows the trade |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Havan kund, wood, ghee, samagri | For the fire |
| Images of Ganesha and Lakshmi | The seat of the pooja |
| Coins, an account book | Kept before the deity |
| Flowers, sweets, incense | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The owner's name and the business are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Havan | Offerings with the Shri Suktam and other mantras |
| Purnahuti | The final offering |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah | For Lakshmi |
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |
