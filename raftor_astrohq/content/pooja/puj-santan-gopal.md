## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Krishna as Bal Gopal |
| What | A pooja and japa of the Santan Gopal mantra |
| When | Thursdays, Ekadashi or Janmashtami |
| Who | The couple |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for a child |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Bal Gopal | The Bhagavata Purana tells of the child Krishna raised by Yashoda and Nanda |
| The prayer | Devotees who wish for a child offer to Bal Gopal as to their own child |
| The mantra | The Santan Gopal mantra asks Krishna, the son of Devaki, for a child |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Thursday, Ekadashi, or Janmashtami |
| Time | Morning after a bath |
| Alongside | Medical advice. A pooja is not a substitute for it |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| An image of Bal Gopal | The seat of the pooja |
| Butter, mishri, milk | The food a child Krishna is offered |
| Tulsi, flowers | Offered |
| Incense, lamp, a small cradle (optional) | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The couple's names and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Bal Gopal pooja | The image is bathed, dressed and offered food |
| Japa | The Santan Gopal mantra is chanted |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Devaki-suta Govinda Vasudeva Jagatpate, dehi me tanayam Krishna tvam aham sharanam gatah | The Santan Gopal mantra |
| Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya | A common Krishna mantra |
