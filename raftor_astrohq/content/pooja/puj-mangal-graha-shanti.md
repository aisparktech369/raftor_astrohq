## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mars (Mangala) |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Mars, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Tuesday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Mangala, son of the Earth | The Puranas call Mars Bhauma, the son of Bhumi (the Earth). He is the planet of courage, drive and the warrior |
| Hanuman and Mars | Hanuman is prayed to on Tuesdays, the day of Mars, for courage |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart and the running period (dasha) |
| Day | Tuesday |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Toor dal | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of red colour | Offered or donated |
| Red flowers, red lentil, jaggery | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Mars is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Tuesday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Kram Krim Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah | The beej mantra of Mars |
| Hanuman Chalisa | A hymn recited with it |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Do I need Mars shanti?</summary><p>That is for <a href="/consult">your astrologer</a> to say, from your birth chart. It is not for everyone.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it the same as wearing the gem of Mars?</summary><p>No. A shanti is a pooja for the planet. The gem is worn. Some families do both on advice. See the Gemstones series.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
