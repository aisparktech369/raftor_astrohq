| When | Saturday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |

| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Shani, son of Surya and Chhaya | Shani is the son of the Sun and his shadow-wife Chhaya, and brother of Yama. One tale (Brahma Vaivarta Purana) has his glance fall on the infant Ganesha. He is the slow planet and the giver of results of past actions |
| Dasharatha's hymn | In the tradition King Dasharatha praised Shani with a hymn that is still recited, the Dashratha Shani Stotra |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Black sesame | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of black or blue colour | Offered or donated |
| Black sesame, sesame-oil lamp, blue flowers | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Saturn is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Saturday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
