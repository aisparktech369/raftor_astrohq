## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, the nine planets and the family deity |
| What | The first shaving of the child's head, leaving a tuft in some families |
| When | The first or third year, on a day from the panchang |
| Who | The family, a priest and a barber |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The sanskar | Chudakarana is one of the sixteen sanskars |
| The meaning | Hair from birth is let go, as a sign of a fresh start for the child |
| The hair | It is offered to a river or buried at the foot of a tree, as the family's custom |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | The first or third year is common, with odd years favoured in many families |
| Day | Taken from the panchang for the child |
| Place | The home, a temple or a river bank |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash, flowers, rice, roli | The seat of the pooja |
| Sandal paste or turmeric | Applied after the haircut |
| Clean cloth, a bowl of water | For the hair and for washing |
| Sweets, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The family's names and the purpose are stated |
| Ganesh and Navagraha pooja | Ganesha and the planets are honoured |
| The haircut | The hair is cut, with a prayer |
| The bath | The child is bathed and dressed in new clothes |
| Offering of the hair | It is offered to the river or buried |
| Prasad | Sweets are shared |
