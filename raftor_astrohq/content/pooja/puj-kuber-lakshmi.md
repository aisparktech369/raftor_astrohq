## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Lakshmi and Kubera |
| What | A pooja with the Shri Suktam and Kubera mantra |
| When | Fridays, Dhanteras, Diwali or by a priest's advice |
| Who | The household |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for wealth |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Lakshmi | The Puranas tell that Lakshmi rose from the churning of the ocean and chose Vishnu |
| Kubera | Kubera is the lord of the yakshas, the guardian of wealth in the north |
| Dhanteras | On the thirteenth tithi of Kartik, Lakshmi and Kubera are honoured before Diwali |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Friday for Lakshmi, Dhanteras for Kubera, or Diwali |
| Time | Evening is common |
| The prayer | For steady means and wise use of them |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Images of Lakshmi and Kubera | The seat of the pooja |
| Lotus or red flowers | For Lakshmi |
| Coins or notes, rice, turmeric | Offered and kept |
| Ghee lamp, incense, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Lakshmi and Kubera | They are invited and offered the upachara |
| Shri Suktam | The hymn to Lakshmi is recited |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah | For Lakshmi |
| Om Yakshaya Kuberaya Vaishravanaya Dhanadhanyadhipataye Dhanadhanyasamriddhim me dehi dapaya svaha | The Kubera mantra |
