## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deities | Lakshmi, with Ganesha worshipped first. Kubera is often added |
| When | The new-moon night (Amavasya) of Kartik, in the evening |
| Who | The household, led by the elders, or a priest |
| Time taken | About one to two hours |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions, with the festival poojas |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Lakshmi from the ocean | The Puranas tell that Lakshmi rose from the churning of the ocean and chose Vishnu |
| Ganesha first | Ganesha is worshipped before every rite, as the remover of obstacles |
| The festival of lamps | Diwali is also linked with the return of Rama to Ayodhya, welcomed with rows of lamps |
| Kubera | Kubera is the keeper of wealth in the tradition and is honoured beside Lakshmi |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | The Amavasya of Kartik. The date changes every year, so check the panchang |
| Time | The evening after sunset (Pradosh kaal) is the most common choice |
| Muhurat | A fixed (sthir) lagna is often chosen. Your town's panchang gives the exact time |
| Place | The house is cleaned and lit before the pooja |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Images or idols of Lakshmi and Ganesha | The seat of the pooja |
| Kalash | A water pot with mango leaves and a coconut |
| Rice, roli (kumkum), turmeric | For the tilak and the offerings |
| Flowers, with lotus if possible | Lakshmi's flower |
| Diyas with ghee or oil | The lamps |
| Kheel and batasha, sweets, fruit | The food offered |
| Coins or notes, betel leaf, incense | Offered and kept |
| Gangajal | Sprinkled to purify |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The performer states the name, family line, place and purpose |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is invited first with a mantra and flowers |
| Kalash sthapana | The water pot is set up as the seat of the divine |
| Lakshmi avahan | Lakshmi is invited and offered the upachara in order |
| Shri Suktam | Verses in praise of Lakshmi are recited, where the family knows them |
| Aarti | A lamp is waved and the Lakshmi aarti is sung |
| Prasad | The food is shared. Lamps are lit around the home |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | A common mantra for Ganesha |
| Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah | A common mantra for Lakshmi |
| Shri Suktam | The Vedic hymn to Lakshmi, recited in full by priests |
