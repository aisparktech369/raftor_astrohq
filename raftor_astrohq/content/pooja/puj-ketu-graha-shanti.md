## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Ketu |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Ketu, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Tuesday or Thursday, by practice, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Svarbhanu and the nectar | In the churning of the ocean the demon Svarbhanu drank the nectar in disguise and Vishnu severed his head. The head is Rahu and the body became Ketu, which stands for detachment and liberation |
| Detachment | Ketu stands for moksha, the letting go of the world, in Jyotish |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart and the running period (dasha) |
| Day | Tuesday or Thursday, by practice |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Horse gram | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of varies colour | Offered or donated |
| Horse gram, a lamp, flowers | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Ketu is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Tuesday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Stram Strim Straum Sah Ketave Namah | The beej mantra of Ketu |
| Ganapati Atharvashirsha | A hymn recited with it |
