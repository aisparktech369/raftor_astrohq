## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | All seven planets between Rahu and Ketu in the chart |
| How it is read | The planets' places from Rahu round to Ketu, with twelve named types in practice |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The names | Twelve types are named in practice, among them Anant, Kulik, Vasuki, Shankhpal, Padma, Mahapadma, Takshak, Karkotak, Shankhachud, Ghatak, Vishdhar and Sheshnag |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Nag Panchami, or a day a priest advises |
| Place | Trimbakeshwar near Nashik and Ujjain are known places for the rite |
| Opinion | Many astrologers see it as a later idea, and others hold it important |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |

| Black sesame, lamp | Offered |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the dosha are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Rahu and Ketu pooja | They and the serpent deities are invited |
| Mantras | Rahu, Ketu and Shiva mantras are chanted |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Daan and prasad | A gift is made and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Bhram Bhrim Bhraum Sah Rahave Namah | The beej mantra of Rahu |
| Om Stram Strim Straum Sah Ketave Namah | The beej mantra of Ketu |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is Kaal Sarp dosh real?</summary><p>Astrologers differ. Some do not use it, and others treat it as important. Ask for the reading.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it need to be done at Trimbakeshwar?</summary><p>Families go there by custom. The pooja can also be done elsewhere, or in your name.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
