## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Saturn moving through the 12th, 1st and 2nd houses from the Moon, over about seven and a half years |
| How it is read | The Moon sign and Saturn's transit |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Saturn's cycle | Saturn takes about twenty-nine and a half years to go round the zodiac, so a Sade Sati comes about every thirty years of life |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Saturday, and Shani Jayanti |
| Count | It runs about seven and a half years. Many people simply continue with work and discipline |
| Opinion | Astrologers see it as testing, not only as bad |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Black sesame, oil, a lamp | Offered to Shani |
| Black or blue cloth, iron | Given in daan |
| Hanuman Chalisa | Recited |
| Flowers, incense | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Shani pooja | Shani is offered the upachara |
| Mantra and Chalisa | The Shani mantra and the Hanuman Chalisa are recited |
| Daan | A gift of oil, sesame or cloth to the needy |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Pram Prim Praum Sah Shanaishcharaya Namah | The beej mantra of Saturn |
| Om Hanumate Namah | For Hanuman |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is Sade Sati always bad?</summary><p>No. Many people do well in it. It is a time of discipline.</p></details>

<details><summary>What helps through it?</summary><p>Steady work, honesty, service of the poor and the elderly are what the tradition advises.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
