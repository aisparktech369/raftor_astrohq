## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha and the family deity, with the Sun and Moon honoured |
| What | The naming of the child |
| When | The eleventh or twelfth day after birth, or a day the priest advises |
| Who | The family, with a priest |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The sixteen sanskars | Naamkaran is one of the sanskars that mark a life, from before birth to the last rite |
| The birth star | The priest takes the child's nakshatra and its quarter (pada), which gives a first syllable for the name |
| The name | The name is whispered in the child's ear and then announced to the family |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | The eleventh or twelfth day is common. Families also choose a later auspicious day |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang and the child's birth details |
| Place | The home, or a temple |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash and a small mandap | The seat of the pooja |
| Rice, flowers, turmeric, roli | For the offerings |
| A plate of rice for writing the name | The name is written on it |
| New clothes for the child | Worn for the rite |
| Sweets, fruit, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The family's names and the purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Kalash and deities | The family deity and the planets are honoured |
| The name | The first syllable is chosen. The father says the name in the child's right ear |
| Blessings | Elders bless the child |
| Prasad | Sweets are shared |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>What if the birth star is not known?</summary><p>Give the time and place of birth to the priest, and they will take it from the panchang.</p></details>

<details><summary>Can the name be chosen by the family?</summary><p>Yes. The priest gives the first syllable, and the family chooses the name.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
