## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of the chart, as most astrologers count |
| How it is read | The house of Mars from the lagna, and from the Moon and Venus in some practice |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Mars and marriage | In Jyotish practice Mars in these houses is held to make the marriage house hot. It matters most when matching two charts |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday |
| Place | Ujjain's Mangalnath temple is the traditional place of Mars worship |
| When | Before the wedding, as advised |
| Cancellation | Many charts have a cancelling rule, and a match with another Manglik is also held to settle it |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Red cloth, red flowers | For Mars |
| Toor dal, jaggery | Offered |
| Kalash, havan items | If a havan is done |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the dosha are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Mangal pooja | Mars is invited and offered the upachara |
| Mantra | The Mars mantra is chanted |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Kram Krim Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah | The beej mantra of Mars |
| Om Angarakaya Namah | A short Mars mantra |
