## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Vastu Purusha, with Ganesha, the nine planets and the guardians of the directions |
| What | A pooja and havan for a home, shop or plot |
| When | On a new building, after repairs, or when the layout is changed |
| Who | A priest, with the owner |
| Time taken | Half a day |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Vastu Purusha | The Matsya Purana tells of Vastu Purusha, a being the gods pinned to the ground, so that every part of a site has a deity in charge |
| The directions | Each direction has a guardian (dikpala), and the north-east is called the place of the divine |
| Vastu shanti | The pooja honours the site so that building and living on it begin in harmony |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Occasion | Griha pravesh, a new shop, or a change such as breaking a wall |
| Day | Taken from the panchang for the owner |
| Place | On the site, with the owner present |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash, coconut, mango leaves | The seat of the divine |
| Mandala cloth or drawing | The Vastu Purusha grid |
| Nine grains | For the nine planets |
| Havan items | Kund, wood, ghee and samagri |
| Flowers, incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The owner's name, family line and the site are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Vastu Purusha pooja | The mandala is worshipped and the directions are invited |
| Navagraha pooja | The nine planets are honoured |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Vastu Purushaya Namah | A common mantra for Vastu Purusha |
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |
