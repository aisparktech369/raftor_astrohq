## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Jupiter conjunct Rahu in the same sign |
| How it is read | The two planets together in a house of the chart |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The reading | In Jyotish practice Jupiter's wisdom is clouded by Rahu. The effect is read by the house and the strength of each planet |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Thursday for Jupiter, Saturday for Rahu |
| Place | The home or a temple |
| Alongside | Respect for teachers and elders |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Yellow flowers, chana dal | For Jupiter |
| Urad dal, blue flowers | For Rahu |
| Kalash, havan items | The usual samagri |
| Incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Guru and Rahu pooja | Both planets are invited |
| Mantras | Their beej mantras are chanted |
| Daan | A gift of yellow cloth or grain |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gram Grim Graum Sah Gurave Namah | For Jupiter |
| Om Bhram Bhrim Bhraum Sah Rahave Namah | For Rahu |
