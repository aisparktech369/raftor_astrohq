## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Radha and Krishna |
| What | A pooja with offerings, bhajan and the divine names |
| When | Fridays, Purnima, Radhashtami or Janmashtami |
| Who | The devotee, or a priest on their behalf |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for love |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Radha | Radha is the beloved of Krishna in the Gita Govinda of Jayadeva and the Brahma Vaivarta Purana |
| Krishna | The Bhagavata Purana tells of Krishna's childhood and his rasa-lila with the gopis |
| The meaning | In devotion the love of Radha and Krishna stands for love that gives itself freely |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Friday, Purnima, Radhashtami (Bhadrapada) or Janmashtami |
| Time | Evening is common, after a bath |
| The prayer | For love and harmony. It never asks for a person to be bound or changed against their will |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Images of Radha and Krishna | The seat of the pooja |
| Tulsi, flowers, peacock feather | Offered |
| Butter, mishri, sweets | The food offered |
| Incense, lamp, flute (if kept) | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Invocation | Radha and Krishna are invited and offered the upachara |
| Bhajan and japa | Names and mantras are sung and chanted |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya | A common Krishna mantra |
| Om Shri Radha-Krishnabhyam Namah | For the divine couple |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Can a pooja make someone love me?</summary><p>No. A pooja cannot bind another person's will, and no honest priest will promise it. It is a prayer for love and harmony.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it for marriage?</summary><p>It is for love in general. For marriage, see the Gauri-Shankar pooja.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
