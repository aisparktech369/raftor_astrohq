## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | A symbolic marriage, as a remedy for Mangal dosh or a similar reading |
| How it is read | The chart, read by [an astrologer](/consult) before the marriage |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The rite | The person is wed in form to a clay pot (kumbh), a peepal tree or a Vishnu image, and the symbolic bond is then ended with prayers |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When | Before the wedding, on a day chosen by the priest |
| Who | The person concerned, with the family |
| An honest note | It is a symbolic act. No real person is married or harmed in it |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| A clay pot, a peepal sapling or an image | The symbolic partner |
| Wedding items: garlands, turmeric, rice | As in a wedding |
| Havan items | The fire offerings |
| Clothes and sweets | For the family |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| The symbolic wedding | The vows are said with the pot, tree or image |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Closing | The symbolic bond is ended with prayers and the pot is immersed |
| Prasad | Prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya | For Vishnu |
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |

<details><summary>Is it a real marriage?</summary><p>No. It is a symbolic rite and has no legal standing.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it needed for every Manglik?</summary><p>No. It is advised by some astrologers. Ask for the reading of your chart.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
