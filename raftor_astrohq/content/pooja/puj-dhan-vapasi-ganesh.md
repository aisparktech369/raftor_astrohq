## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, with Lakshmi and Kubera |
| What | A pooja with Ganesh mantras and a prayer for the return of money |
| When | Wednesdays and Chaturthi |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for money that is stuck |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Ganesha | Ganesha is the remover of obstacles, and is prayed to when a thing is stuck |
| Lakshmi and Kubera | They are honoured for the steady flow of money |
| The meaning | The prayer is for steadiness while you act. It cannot compel another to pay |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Wednesday or Chaturthi |
| Time | Morning after a bath |
| Alongside | A written demand, records, and a lawyer if needed |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| An image of Ganesha | The seat of the pooja |
| Durva, red flowers, sindoor | Offered |
| Modak or laddu | The food offered |
| Incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is offered the upachara |
| Japa | Ganesh mantras are chanted |
| Lakshmi and Kubera | They are honoured |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Will my money come back?</summary><p>No pooja can promise that. Keep records and ask in writing. A lawyer can advise.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it the same as Rin-harta Ganesh?</summary><p>It is related. That one is for your own debt, and this is for money owed to you.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
