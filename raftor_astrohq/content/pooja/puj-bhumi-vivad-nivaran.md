## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Bhumi Devi (the Earth), Mars (Mangala) and Ganesha |
| What | A pooja and havan |
| When | Tuesdays, or a day from the panchang |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | Two to three hours |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for a property dispute |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Bhumi Devi | The Earth is worshipped as a goddess, and the Puranas call Mars her son |
| Mangala | Mars stands for land and property in Jyotish |
| The meaning | The prayer asks for steadiness in a long dispute |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday for Mars |
| Place | The home, or at the land if the owner wishes |
| Alongside | A lawyer, the papers and honest advice |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Soil or a clay image for Bhumi | The seat of the pooja |
| Red flowers, red lentil | For Mars |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Bhumi and Mangala pooja | The Earth and Mars are offered the upachara |
| Havan | Offerings into the fire |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Bhumi Devyai Namah | For Bhumi Devi |
| Om Angarakaya Namah | For Mars |
