## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Shiva and Parvati (Gauri) |
| What | A pooja, often with a fast, for a good marriage |
| When | Mondays, Hartalika Teej, or by a priest's advice |
| Who | The one seeking marriage, or the family |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for marriage |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Parvati's penance | The Puranas tell that Parvati performed severe penance to win Shiva as her husband |
| The Kumarasambhava | Kalidasa's poem tells of the same story and of the wedding of Shiva and Parvati |
| The Monday vows | Many keep sixteen Mondays (Solah Somvar) with prayer to Shiva and Parvati |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Monday, Hartalika Teej (Bhadrapada) or a day the priest advises |
| Time | Morning or evening, after a bath |
| The prayer | For a good match and a lasting marriage |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Images of Shiva and Parvati | The seat of the pooja |
| Bilva leaves, white flowers | For Shiva |
| Red flowers, sindoor, bangles | For Gauri |
| Water, milk | For the abhishek |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Shiva abhishek | The lingam is bathed |
| Gauri pooja | Parvati is offered the upachara and the marks of marriage |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namah Shivaya | For Shiva |
| Om Gauri Shankaraya Namah | For the divine couple |
