## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Sun or Moon with Rahu or Ketu, or birth during an eclipse |
| How it is read | The Sun or Moon with a node in the chart |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Eclipses | The Puranas tell that Rahu swallows the Sun and Moon at an eclipse, after the story of Svarbhanu |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Sunday for the Sun, Monday for the Moon, or an eclipse day |
| Eclipse time | Many families bathe, chant and give gifts during an eclipse, and avoid eating |
| Alongside | The Surya and Chandra graha shanti |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Wheat, red flowers | For the Sun |
| Rice, white flowers, milk | For the Moon |
| Black sesame, a lamp | For Rahu or Ketu |
| Kalash, incense | The usual samagri |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Pooja | The Sun or Moon and the node are invited |
| Mantras | Their mantras are chanted |
| Daan | A gift of grain or cloth |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Suryaya Namah | For the Sun |
| Om Som Somaya Namah | For the Moon |
