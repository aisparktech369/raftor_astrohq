## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Mercury (Budha) |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Mercury, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Wednesday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Budha, son of the Moon | The Puranas tell that Budha was born to Chandra and Tara, and was the father of Pururavas, from whom the lunar dynasty descends |
| Vishnu | The planet Budha is linked in practice with Vishnu, and his name is recited on Wednesdays |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart and the running period (dasha) |
| Day | Wednesday |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Moong | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of green colour | Offered or donated |
| Green cloth, tulsi, green moong | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Mercury is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Wednesday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Bram Brim Braum Sah Budhaya Namah | The beej mantra of Mercury |
| Vishnu Sahasranama | A hymn recited with it |
