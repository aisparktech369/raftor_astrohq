## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deities | The nine planets (Navagraha) |
| What | A pooja or havan for the nine planets together, or for one |
| When | When astrologers advise it, such as a difficult planetary period, or at Griha Pravesh |
| Who | A priest |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The nine planets | Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu are worshipped as the nine planets |
| Rahu and Ketu | The Puranas tell of the demon Svarbhanu who drank the nectar and was severed by Vishnu. His head is Rahu and his body is Ketu |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## The nine planets

| Planet | Day | Grain offered | Colour of cloth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun (Surya) | Sunday | Wheat | Red |
| Moon (Chandra) | Monday | Rice | White |
| Mars (Mangala) | Tuesday | Toor dal | Red |
| Mercury (Budha) | Wednesday | Moong | Green |
| Jupiter (Guru) | Thursday | Chana | Yellow |
| Venus (Shukra) | Friday | White beans | White |
| Saturn (Shani) | Saturday | Black sesame | Black or blue |
| Rahu | Saturday | Urad | Dark blue or black |
| Ketu | Tuesday or Thursday | Horse gram | Varies |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart and the running period (dasha) |
| For one planet | A shanti for the day of that planet |
| For all nine | Navagraha shanti is also done at Griha Pravesh |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Nine heaps of grain | One for each planet, on a cloth |
| Kalash, flowers, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Cloth in each planet's colour | Offered or donated |
| Havan items | If a havan is done with it |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet or planets are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Each planet is invited in its place on the mandala |
| Mantras | The beej mantra of each planet is chanted, often a set number of times |
| Havan | An offering for each planet, if a havan is done |
| Daan | A donation of the planet's grain or cloth, to someone in need |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Do I need Navagraha shanti?</summary><p>That is for <a href="/consult">your astrologer</a>, from your birth chart. It is not needed for everyone.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is it the same as wearing a gemstone?</summary><p>No. A shanti is a pooja for the planet. A gemstone is worn in daily life. Some families do both, on <a href="/consult">an astrologer's</a> advice. See the Gemstones series for the gem of each planet.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
