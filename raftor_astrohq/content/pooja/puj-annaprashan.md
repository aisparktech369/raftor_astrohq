## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, Vishnu and Lakshmi, or the family deity |
| What | The first feeding of grain, usually a rice pudding |
| When | About the sixth month, on a day taken from the panchang |
| Who | The parents, with a priest |
| Time taken | One to two hours |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The sanskar | Annaprashan is one of the sixteen sanskars, the first step to food beyond milk |
| The food | Kheer, the sweet rice, is made with ghee and offered to the deity first |
| The feeding | The father or an elder feeds the baby with a silver or gold spoon |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | About the sixth month. Families differ by the child's sex and month |
| Day | Taken from the panchang |
| Place | The home, or a temple |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Rice, milk, ghee, sugar | For the kheer |
| Silver or gold spoon and a plate | For the feeding |
| Kalash, flowers, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| New clothes for the baby | Worn for the rite |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The parents' names and the purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Offering | The kheer is offered to the deity |
| The first feeding | The child is fed a little of the kheer by the father, then by elders |
| Blessings | Relatives bless the child and give small gifts |
| Prasad | The kheer is shared |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is six months fixed?</summary><p>It is the common age. Ask your doctor about when your baby is ready for solid food. The ceremony follows the baby, not the other way round.</p></details>

<details><summary>Can it be done at home?</summary><p>Yes. Many families do it at home with a priest or an elder.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
