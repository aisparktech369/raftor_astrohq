## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Hanuman |
| What | Recitation of the Chalisa, often 11 or 108 times, with a pooja |
| When | Tuesdays and Saturdays |
| Who | The devotee, or a priest on their behalf |
| Time taken | About an hour, more for many recitations |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for protection |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The author | The Chalisa is by Tulsidas, the poet of the Ramcharitmanas, written in Awadhi |
| Hanuman | In the Ramayana Hanuman is the devoted servant of Rama, strong and humble |
| Forty verses | Chalisa means forty. The hymn has forty verses and a closing couplet |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday and Saturday |
| Time | Morning or evening, after a bath |
| Count | Once daily, or 11 or 108 times for a vow |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| An image of Hanuman or Rama | The seat of the pooja |
| Sindoor and jasmine oil | Offered to Hanuman |
| Red flowers, boondi or laddu | Offered |
| Incense, lamp, a Chalisa book | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Hanuman pooja | Hanuman is offered the upachara |
| Chalisa path | The hymn is recited the chosen number of times |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Hanumate Namah | A common mantra for Hanuman |
| Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar | The first line of the Chalisa |
