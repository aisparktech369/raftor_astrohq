## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What | The first entry into a new home, with a pooja and a havan |
| When | A day taken from the panchang for the family |
| Who | The household, with a priest |
| Time taken | Half a day, with the havan |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Three kinds | Apurva, a new home. Sapurva, a return to a home left for a time. Dvandva, a return after a long absence |
| Vastu Purusha | Vastu tradition tells of the spirit who lies beneath every site, and is honoured so the home begins in harmony |
| Entering with the kalash | A water pot is carried in, as a sign of abundance |
| The boiling of milk | Many families boil milk in the new kitchen so it overflows, as a sign of plenty |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day and month | Taken from the panchang. Chaturmas and Pitru Paksha are generally avoided |
| Time | A fixed (sthir) lagna is often chosen, for the family's own chart |
| Before | The home is cleaned, and a small pooja is done at the threshold |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash, mango leaves, coconut | Carried in and set up |
| Toran, rangoli | Mango leaf string at the door and a design at the threshold |
| Images of Ganesha and the family deity | The seat of the pooja |
| Havan items | Kund, wood, ghee and samagri |
| Grains for the nine planets | For the Navagraha pooja |
| Milk, rice, jaggery, sweets | For the kitchen and the prasad |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and home are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Punyahavachan | Water is sprinkled and blessed to purify the home |
| Vastu and Navagraha pooja | Vastu Purusha and the nine planets are honoured |
| Havan | A fire offering for the home |
| Entry | The family enters with the kalash, the right foot first |
| Milk and prasad | Milk is boiled in the kitchen and prasad is shared |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Can we move in before the pooja?</summary><p>Families differ. Many wait for the muhurat so the first entry is the pooja.</p></details>

<details><summary>Do we need a priest?</summary><p>For the full procedure with the havan, usually yes.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
