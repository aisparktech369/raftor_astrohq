## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Shiva, as Tryambaka and Mrityunjaya |
| What | The mantra chanted a set number of times, often with a havan |
| When | Mondays, Pradosh, Shravan, or by a priest's advice |
| Who | A priest, or the devotee |
| Shelf | Kamna purti, for health |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The Rigveda | The mantra is from the Rigveda, 7.59.12, and appears in the Yajurveda too |
| Markandeya | The Puranas tell of the boy Markandeya, who held to Shiva and was saved from Yama |
| The meaning | The verse asks to be freed from death "like a cucumber from its stem", not from life |
| Not a cure | It is a prayer. Medical care must continue |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Monday, the day of Shiva |
| Count | A mala of 108 is common. Larger numbers, up to 125,000, are done by priests over days |
| Time | Early morning or evening, after a bath, facing east or north |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Image or lingam of Shiva | The seat of the jaap |
| Rudraksha mala | To count the mantra |
| Water, bilva leaves, white flowers | Offered |
| Lamp, incense | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done at the end |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Shiva pooja | A short pooja to Shiva |
| Jaap | The mantra is chanted the set number of times |
| Havan | A tenth of the count is often offered into the fire |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantra

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Tryambakam yajamahe sugandhim pushtivardhanam, urvarukamiva bandhanan mrityor mukshiya maamritat | The Maha Mrityunjaya mantra |
| Om Namah Shivaya | The five-syllable mantra, as a shorter one |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Can it cure illness?</summary><p>No. It is a prayer, not a treatment. Continue medical care and follow your doctor's advice.</p></details>

<details><summary>How many times should it be chanted?</summary><p>A mala of 108 is common. Larger counts are done by priests over several days.</p></details>

<details><summary>Can it be done in my name?</summary><p>Yes. The priest states your name and family line. Ask how it will be shared with you.</p></details>
