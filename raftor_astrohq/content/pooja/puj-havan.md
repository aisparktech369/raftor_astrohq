## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Agni, who carries the offering to the gods |
| What | Offerings poured into a sacred fire with mantras |
| Also called | Homa, yajna |
| Who | A priest, with the family seated around |
| Time taken | One to three hours, or longer for large havans |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Agni in the Rigveda | The first hymn of the Rigveda is to Agni, the fire that carries the offering |
| The word swaha | Each offering ends with swaha, the call that sends it to the deity |
| A rite of purification | Many families do a havan to bless a home, a business or a new beginning |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Occasion | Griha Pravesh, a birth, a wedding, a festival, or a planet's shanti |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |
| Place | A clean open or ventilated place, with the fire set safely |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Havan kund | The fire pit, often a square one of copper or brick |
| Wood (samidha) | Mango wood is common, and other woods are used for some havans |
| Ghee | Poured on the fire with a ladle |
| Havan samagri | A herbal and grain mixture, offered with each mantra |
| Camphor, kindling | To light the fire |
| Coconut | The final offering (purnahuti) |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Lighting the fire | The fire is lit with camphor and kindling, with a mantra |
| Ahuti | Ghee and samagri are offered with each mantra, ending with swaha. Many count 108 |
| Purnahuti | The final offering of a coconut, with ghee |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |
| Closing | The fire is allowed to die down by itself |

## Types

| Havan | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ganapati havan | A beginning, free of obstacles |
| Navagraha havan | The nine planets, as part of a shanti |
| Maha Mrityunjaya havan | A prayer for health and long life |
| Lakshmi havan | A prayer for prosperity |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is a havan safe indoors?</summary><p>Only with care. Keep it in a ventilated place, away from curtains, and have water at hand. Many people do it in an open courtyard or a hall.</p></details>

<details><summary>What does swaha mean?</summary><p>It is the call that ends each offering and sends it to the deity.</p></details>

<details><summary>Can a havan be done in my name?</summary><p>Yes. The priest states your name and family line in the sankalp. Ask how it will be shared with you.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does a havan guarantee a result?</summary><p>No. It is an act of devotion and not a guaranteed outcome.</p></details>
