## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | No planet in the 2nd or 12th house from the Moon, nor with it |
| How it is read | The planets round the Moon in the chart |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The reading | The Moon stands alone, which the tradition reads as a feeling of lacking support. Many charts have rules that cancel it |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Monday |
| Place | A Shiva temple, or the home |
| Alongside | Friends, family and a steady routine |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| White flowers, rice, milk | For the Moon |
| Water, bilva | For the Shiva abhishek |
| Silver, white cloth | Offered or given |
| Incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Chandra pooja | The Moon is invited and offered the upachara |
| Mantras | The Moon and Shiva mantras are chanted |
| Daan | A gift of rice or milk |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Shram Shrim Shraum Sah Chandramase Namah | For the Moon |
| Om Namah Shivaya | For Shiva |
