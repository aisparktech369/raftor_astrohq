## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Agni as the witness, with Ganesha, the nine planets and the family deities |
| What | The wedding rites, from the pooja to the seven steps |
| When | On a muhurat from the panchang and the couple's charts |
| Who | The couple, their families and priests |
| Time taken | Several hours, and more with the customs of the region |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Agni as witness | The couple take their vows before the sacred fire |
| Saptapadi | The seven steps taken together, each with a vow, make the marriage binding in the tradition |
| The hymn | The Vivah Sukta of the Rigveda (10.85) is the old hymn of the wedding |
| Many customs | Haldi, mehndi, baraat and others differ by region and community |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang. The charts of the couple are matched first |
| Matching | The kundli match (Ashtakoota) and dosha checks are done before the date |
| Place | The wedding venue, with the fire set safely |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Kalash, mango leaves, coconut | The seat of the divine |
| Havan items | Kund, wood, ghee and samagri |
| Mangalsutra, sindoor, garlands | The marks of the marriage |
| Rice, flowers, turmeric, roli | For the offerings |
| Clothes and gifts | As the families' customs |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | Both families' names and the purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Kanyadaan | The bride's parents give her hand |
| Agni and the vows | The fire is lit and the vows are taken |
| Saptapadi | The seven steps, with the vows |
| Mangalsutra and sindoor | The marks of marriage are given |
| Blessings | Elders bless the couple |
