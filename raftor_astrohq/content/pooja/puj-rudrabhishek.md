## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Shiva, as Rudra |
| What | Ritual bathing of the lingam while the Rudram is recited |
| When | Mondays, Pradosh (the thirteenth tithi), Mahashivaratri, and the month of Shravan |
| Who | A priest, or the devotee if trained |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The Rudram | The Rudram (Namakam and Chamakam) is a hymn to Rudra in the Yajurveda, recited during the abhishek |
| The poison of the ocean | The Puranas tell that Shiva drank the poison Halahala that rose in the churning of the ocean. Devotees bathe the lingam to cool and honour him |
| Rudra and Shiva | Rudra is the Vedic name. Shiva, the auspicious one, is the name that grew in later tradition |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weekday | Monday, the day of Shiva |
| Tithi | Pradosh, in the evening of the thirteenth tithi, and Mahashivaratri |
| Month | Shravan is a favoured month |
| Muhurat | Your town's panchang gives the exact time |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Water, Gangajal | First and last bath |
| Milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugar | The five (panchamrit) offerings, one after another |
| Bilva leaves | Offered to Shiva |
| White flowers, sandal paste | Offered after the bath |
| Vibhuti (ash) | Applied as a mark |
| Incense, lamp, fruit | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and purpose are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Abhishek | The lingam is bathed with water, then the five offerings, then water again |
| Rudram | The Rudram is recited during the bath |
| Decoration | The lingam is wiped, anointed and given bilva and flowers |
| Aarti | The lamp is waved and Shiva's aarti is sung |
| Prasad | The prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namah Shivaya | The five-syllable mantra of Shiva |
| Maha Mrityunjaya mantra | A mantra from the Rigveda in praise of Shiva |
| Shri Rudram | The hymn recited by priests during the abhishek |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Why is milk poured on Shiva?</summary><p>Tradition links it with Shiva's drinking of the poison in the churning of the ocean. Devotees offer cooling things.</p></details>

<details><summary>Can women perform it?</summary><p>Practices differ by family and lineage. Ask your priest or family elders.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does Rudrabhishek guarantee a result?</summary><p>No. It is an act of devotion and not a guaranteed outcome.</p></details>
