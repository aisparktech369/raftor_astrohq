## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Saturn conjunct the Moon, or in a mutual aspect, in some practice |
| How it is read | The Moon and Saturn together in a house of the chart |
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart |
| Time taken | One to three hours, more for large rites |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, doshas and yogas |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| The reading | Vish means poison. The Moon stands for the mind and Saturn for weight, so the yoga is read as a heavy, serious mind |
| A dosha is not a verdict | Astrologers differ on how much weight a dosha carries, and many charts carry rules that cancel it (parihar). Ask for the reading, not a fear |
| The pooja | A pooja or havan asks for peace from the influence. It is a prayer and not a cure |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Monday for the Moon, Saturday for Saturn |
| Place | A Shiva temple, or the home |
| Alongside | Care for the mind. If low mood persists, see a doctor |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Water, milk, bilva | For the Shiva abhishek |
| Black sesame, oil lamp | For Shani |
| White flowers | For the Moon |
| Incense, lamp | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the matter are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Shiva abhishek | The lingam is bathed |
| Mantras | Shiva, Moon and Saturn mantras are chanted |
| Daan | A gift of rice or sesame |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Namah Shivaya | For Shiva |
| Om Pram Prim Praum Sah Shanaishcharaya Namah | For Saturn |
