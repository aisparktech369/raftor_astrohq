## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Hanuman |
| What | Recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa or Bajrang Baan, with a pooja |
| When | Tuesdays and Saturdays |
| Who | The devotee, or a priest for them |
| Time taken | About an hour |
| Shelf | Samasya samadhan, for the evil eye |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Nazar | In India it is believed that envious looks can harm a person, a child or a home |
| Customs | Black thread, a lemon and chilli, or a black mark are used in many homes |
| Hanuman | Hanuman is called Sankat Mochan, the remover of troubles, and is prayed to for protection |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | Tuesday and Saturday |
| Time | Morning or evening |
| A note | Nazar is a belief. A child who is unwell needs a doctor |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Image of Hanuman | The seat of the pooja |
| Sindoor, jasmine oil, red flowers | Offered |
| Salt, mustard seed, dried chilli | Used in the nazar-removal custom of some families |
| Incense, lamp, boondi | The usual upachara |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name and the aim are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Hanuman pooja | Hanuman is offered the upachara |
| Path | The Hanuman Chalisa is recited |
| Nazar custom | The family's custom is done, if any |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
