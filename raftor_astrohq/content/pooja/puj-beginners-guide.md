## What it is

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is a pooja? | An act of worship in which a deity is invited, honoured with offerings and thanked |
| Who performs it? | You, at home, or a priest on your behalf. Both are traditional |
| Is a result guaranteed? | No. A pooja is an act of devotion, and no result can be promised |
| Do customs differ? | Yes. Steps, mantras and items vary by region, family and lineage |

## What a pooja is, and what we are

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | An act of seeking blessings (ashirwad) for an aim: a wish fulfilled (kamna purti), a trouble eased, a new beginning made well |
| Who gives the blessing | The divine. The blessing is received, and cannot be made or bought |
| Who performs the rite | A priest, in your name. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it |
| What no one can do | Promise the result. Your own effort and your faith go with the rite |

## Pooja, havan, abhishek, jaap

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Pooja | Worship with offerings, lamp and aarti before an image or symbol |
| Havan (homa) | Offerings made into a sacred fire with mantras |
| Abhishek | Ritual bathing of the deity, often a Shiva lingam, with water, milk and other things |
| Jaap | Repeating a mantra a set number of times, often counted on a mala |
| Katha | Reading or hearing a sacred story, often with a pooja, as in Satyanarayan |
| Shanti | A pooja or havan done to pacify a planet or an influence |

## The steps most poojas follow

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The resolve: the performer states the name, family line (gotra), place, date and purpose |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first, to begin without obstacles |
| Kalash | A water pot with leaves and a coconut is set up as the seat of the divine |
| Avahan | The deity is invited |
| Upachara | Offerings in order: seat, water, bath, cloth, sandal paste, flowers, incense, lamp and food. Many count sixteen, and lists differ |
| Aarti | A lamp is waved before the deity with a song of praise |
| Pushpanjali and pradakshina | A handful of flowers is offered and the deity is circled |
| Prasad | The food offered is shared |
| Kshama prarthana | Pardon is asked for any mistake |

## How poojas are arranged here

| Shelf | For | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Sanskar and occasions | The sanskars and occasions of a household | Griha pravesh, Satyanarayan katha, naming, first rice, a new shop or vehicle |
| Kamna purti | A wish, by what is wished for | Love, marriage, child, wealth, health, education, career, peace, protection |
| Samasya samadhan | A problem, by what troubles | Court case, business loss, debt, evil eye, a family or property dispute |
| Graha shanti | The planets and doshas | Navagraha, each planet, Kaal Sarp, Pitra dosh, Sade Sati |

## When to choose a pooja

| Occasion | Common choice |
|---|---|
| New home | Griha Pravesh with Vastu pooja and havan |
| New business, vehicle | A Ganesh and Lakshmi pooja |
| A planet's period | Navagraha shanti |
| Devotion to Shiva | Rudrabhishek, on Mondays, Pradosh or Shivaratri |
| A festival | The festival's own pooja, such as Lakshmi pooja at Diwali |
| Gratitude, family gathering | Satyanarayan katha and pooja |

## Choosing a day and time

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Muhurat | The auspicious time is taken from the panchang for your place |
| Tithi and weekday | Each deity has favoured days, for example Monday for Shiva and Thursday for Vishnu |
| Place | Dates and timings differ by city, so give your town |

## What to ask before you book

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Who performs it | A trained priest who knows the procedure and mantras |
| What is included | The items (samagri), the priest's fee and the time it takes |
| Sankalp details | Name, gotra and place of the person it is for |
| Language | Mantras are in Sanskrit. Ask for the explanation in a language you follow |
| In person or in your name | A pooja can be done in your name when you cannot attend. Ask what proof is shared |
