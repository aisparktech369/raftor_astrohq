## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deity | Ganesha, and the family deity |
| What | A short pooja for a new car, bike or truck |
| When | On the day of delivery or the first use, at an auspicious hour |
| Who | The owner, with a priest |
| Time taken | About thirty minutes |
| Shelf | Sanskar and occasions |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| A blessing for the road | The vehicle is a new companion, so it is blessed before it goes out |
| Ganesha | Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, is honoured first |
| Ayudha pooja | On Dussehra and Navratri, tools and vehicles are worshipped in many regions |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Day | The day of delivery, or a day from the panchang |
| Time | An auspicious hour is chosen |
| Place | Where the vehicle stands, with the engine off |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Roli, rice, flowers | A tilak and a swastik are made |
| A coconut | To be offered and broken |
| Incense, lamp, sweets | The usual upachara |
| A garland and kalash water | For the vehicle |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The owner's name and the vehicle are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| The vehicle | Water is sprinkled, a swastik is drawn and the garland is placed |
| The coconut | It is offered and broken, with a prayer |
| Aarti | The lamp is waved before the vehicle |
| First drive | The vehicle is taken a short way, and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gam Ganapataye Namah | For Ganesha |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Does a pooja make a vehicle safe?</summary><p>No. It is a prayer. Safety comes from careful driving, a good vehicle and rest.</p></details>

<details><summary>Why lemons?</summary><p>In some regions a lemon is placed under a wheel. It is a custom, not a rule.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee a result?</summary><p>No one can promise a result. A pooja is an act of seeking blessings. The priest and AstroHQ are only the means (nimit) of offering it, and what is received rests with the divine.</p></details>
