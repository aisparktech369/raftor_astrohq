## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Rahu |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Rahu, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Saturday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| What it seeks | Blessings (ashirwad) for this aim. The result rests with the divine, and no one can promise it |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Svarbhanu and the nectar | In the churning of the ocean the demon Svarbhanu drank the nectar in disguise. The Sun and Moon exposed him and Vishnu severed his head. The head is Rahu, which is said to swallow them in eclipses |
| Eclipses | Rahu is said to swallow the Sun and Moon at an eclipse, as the head of Svarbhanu |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Urad | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of dark blue or black colour | Offered or donated |
| Urad dal, blue flowers, a lamp | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Rahu is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Saturday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Bhram Bhrim Bhraum Sah Rahave Namah | The beej mantra of Rahu |
| Durga Saptashati verses | A hymn recited with it |
