## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Planet | Jupiter (Guru, Brihaspati) |
| What | A pooja and often a havan for Jupiter, with its mantra chanted |
| When | Thursday, or by [an astrologer's](/consult) advice during its period |
| Who | A priest, for the person |
| Time taken | One to three hours |
| Shelf | Graha shanti, the planets |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Story and meaning

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Brihaspati, teacher of the gods | Brihaspati, son of the sage Angiras, is the guru of the devas and counsellor of Indra. He stands for wisdom, teaching and fortune |
| Thursday | Thursday is the day of Jupiter, and his hymns and mantras are recited on it |
| Shanti | The word means peace. The rite asks for peace from a planet's difficult effects, not for a result |

## When to perform

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who advises it | [An astrologer](/consult), from the birth chart and the running period (dasha) |
| Day | Thursday |
| Muhurat | Taken from the panchang for your town |

## Items (samagri)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| Chana dal | The grain of the planet, in a heap on a cloth |
| Cloth of yellow colour | Offered or donated |
| Yellow flowers, turmeric, chana dal | Offered |
| Kalash, incense, lamp | The usual upachara |
| Havan items | If a havan is done |

## Steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sankalp | The name, family line and the planet are stated |
| Ganesh pooja | Ganesha is honoured first |
| Graha avahan | Jupiter is invited in its place |
| Mantra | The beej mantra is chanted a set number of times, often by several priests |
| Daan | The grain or cloth is given to someone in need, on Thursday |
| Aarti and prasad | The lamp is waved and prasad is shared |

## Mantras

| Mantra | Use |
|---|---|
| Om Gram Grim Graum Sah Gurave Namah | The beej mantra of Jupiter |
| Guru Stotra | A hymn recited with it |
