## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Pearl, Moti |
| Mineral | Calcium carbonate (nacre) formed in a mollusc. An organic gem |
| Hardness | 2.5 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale. Soft |
| Traditional association | The Moon (Chandra) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Japan, China | Akoya pearls, small, round and bright. China is the main freshwater source |
| Australia, Indonesia, Philippines | South Sea pearls, large, white to gold |
| French Polynesia | Tahitian pearls, dark with green and purple overtones |
| India | Cultured and freshwater work. Gulf of Mannar is a historic natural source |
| Persian Gulf | The historic source of natural pearls. Natural pearls are now rare |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural | Formed without human help. Rare and costly. Needs a laboratory report |
| Cultured | A nucleus is placed in the mollusc by people. A real pearl, sold as cultured |
| Treated | Bleached, dyed, coated or irradiated for colour. Must be stated |
| Imitation | Glass or plastic beads coated to look like pearls |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links pearl with the Moon, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Chandra and the churning of the ocean | In the Puranas the Moon rose from the Samudra Manthan, the churning of the ocean by gods and demons. Shiva wears its crescent on his head (Chandrashekhara) |
| The Swati raindrop | Indian poetic lore says a raindrop falling in the Swati nakshatra into an oyster becomes a pearl |

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When astrologers consider it | When the planet is judged friendly to your birth chart (by the ascendant) and weak or badly placed, or when its major period is running. It is decided from the chart, not from the sun sign alone |
| The rule behind it | A gem is held to strengthen its planet, whatever the planet signifies for you. A planet that is difficult for your chart is usually not strengthened |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Monday, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: little finger. Metal: silver |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | A short trial is optional. Remove it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: the mind, emotional balance and the mother |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask [your astrologer](/consult). Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Natural or cultured, in words | Only a laboratory can tell with X-ray |
| Treatment | Bleached, dyed or coated |
| Type and size | Akoya, South Sea, Tahitian or freshwater, in millimetres |
| Care | Keep away from perfume, acid and rubbing. Wipe with a soft cloth |
