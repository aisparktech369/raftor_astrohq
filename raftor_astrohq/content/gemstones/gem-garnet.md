## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Garnet |
| Mineral | A group of silicate minerals. Red garnet is usually almandine or pyrope |
| Hardness | 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale |
| Traditional association | Not one of the nine Navaratna. Some practitioners use red garnet as an alternative to ruby, and others do not |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| India | Rajasthan and the south have long supplied garnet |
| Sri Lanka | A source of many garnet types |
| Tanzania, Mozambique, Madagascar | Modern sources |
| Brazil, United States | Further sources |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Garnet is nearly always untreated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown garnet exists, mostly for industry |
| Look-alikes | Red glass and synthetic spinel are different materials |

## How astrologers decide

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When astrologers consider it | When the planet is judged friendly to your birth chart (by the ascendant) and weak or badly placed, or when its major period is running. It is decided from the chart, not from the sun sign alone |
| The rule behind it | A gem is held to strengthen its planet, whatever the planet signifies for you. A planet that is difficult for your chart is usually not strengthened |
| Cautions in practice | Because practices differ, ask [your astrologer](/consult) which planet the stone is meant to serve |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | No classical themes are attached to this stone. Ask your astrologer what, if anything, it is advised for |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by your astrologer from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.
