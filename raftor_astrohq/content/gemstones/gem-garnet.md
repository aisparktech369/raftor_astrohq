## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Garnet |
| Mineral | A group of silicate minerals. Red garnet is usually almandine or pyrope |
| Hardness | 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale |
| Traditional association | Not one of the nine Navaratna. Some practitioners use red garnet as an alternative to ruby, and others do not |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| India | Rajasthan and the south have long supplied garnet |
| Sri Lanka | A source of many garnet types |
| Tanzania, Mozambique, Madagascar | Modern sources |
| Brazil, United States | Further sources |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Garnet is nearly always untreated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown garnet exists, mostly for industry |
| Look-alikes | Red glass and synthetic spinel are different materials |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Garnet is not in the Navaratna. Some practice uses red garnet as a lower-cost alternative to ruby. Ask your astrologer |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States the species, such as almandine or pyrope |
| Species, not just "garnet" | The group has many types and prices |
| Weight in carats | Compare price per carat |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is garnet the same as ruby?</summary><p>No. It is a different mineral and costs far less.</p></details>

<details><summary>Is garnet one of the nine gems?</summary><p>No. Hessonite (Gomed) is a garnet and is one of the nine.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee the traditional benefit people talk about?</summary><p>No. Traditional associations are matters of belief, not guaranteed outcomes.</p></details>
