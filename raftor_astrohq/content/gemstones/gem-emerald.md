## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Emerald, Panna |
| Mineral | Beryl (beryllium aluminium silicate). Green from chromium or vanadium |
| Hardness | 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale, but brittle because of natural fractures |
| Traditional association | Mercury (Budh) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Colombia (Muzo, Chivor, Coscuez) | The historic benchmark. A warm, vivid green |
| Zambia (Kagem) | A major modern source. Often a cooler, bluish green, clean |
| Brazil | Lighter to medium green, in many mines |
| Afghanistan (Panjshir), Pakistan (Swat) | Fine stones from the north. Quality varies |
| Zimbabwe (Sandawana) | Small stones, strong green |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, oiled or resin-filled | Nearly universal. Fills surface cracks. Reports grade it from none to significant |
| Natural, untreated | Rare and costly |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown emerald, same chemistry. Not natural |
| Look-alikes | Green glass, dyed quartz, doublets (two parts joined) and green beryl that is too pale to be called emerald |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links emerald with Mercury, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic, treatment level and weight |
| The treatment level in words | None, minor, moderate or significant changes the value |
| Origin as the report's opinion | Not the seller's say-so |
| Care instructions | Heat, steam, solvents and ultrasonic cleaners can damage a filled stone |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is an oiled emerald fake?</summary><p>No. Oiling is normal for emerald. What matters is that the level is stated on the report.</p></details>

<details><summary>Which planet is emerald linked with?</summary><p>Mercury, in Jyotish practice.</p></details>

<details><summary>Why do emeralds have visible marks inside?</summary><p>Natural inclusions are normal in emerald. A completely clean emerald is rare, and a perfectly clean one sold cheaply should be checked for synthetic or glass.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does it guarantee the traditional benefit people talk about?</summary><p>No. Traditional associations are matters of belief, not guaranteed outcomes.</p></details>
