## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Ruby, Manikya |
| Mineral | Corundum (aluminium oxide). Red from chromium |
| Hardness | 9 on the Mohs scale. Durable for daily wear |
| Traditional association | The Sun (Surya) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Myanmar (Mogok, Mong Hsu) | The historic benchmark. The finest are called "pigeon's blood" |
| Mozambique (Montepuez) | A large modern source since 2009. Good red, often with fine inclusions |
| Thailand, Cambodia | Darker red, often with brownish tone |
| Sri Lanka | Pinkish to light red, often called pink sapphire in trade |
| Madagascar, Tanzania | Mixed quality. A source of much glass-filled material |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, heated | Common. Heat improves colour and clarity |
| Glass-filled | Cracks filled with lead glass. Cheap and common. Can be damaged by acids and heat. It must be stated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown ruby. Same chemistry, not natural |
| Look-alikes | Red spinel and garnet are different minerals. Red glass is an imitation |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links ruby with the Sun, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Surya, the Sun god | Surya rides a chariot of seven horses driven by Aruna, and is the source of light and life in the Vedas and Puranas |
| The king of gems | Ruby is called Ratnaraj, the king of gems, in Indian tradition, and the Garuda Purana and Brihat Samhita describe its qualities |
| The Kaustubha jewel | The jewel Kaustubha, worn by Vishnu, rose from the churning of the ocean. It is often described as a ruby |

## How astrologers decide

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When astrologers consider it | When the planet is judged friendly to your birth chart (by the ascendant) and weak or badly placed, or when its major period is running. It is decided from the chart, not from the sun sign alone |
| The rule behind it | A gem is held to strengthen its planet, whatever the planet signifies for you. A planet that is difficult for your chart is usually not strengthened |
| Cautions in practice | Many astrologers advise against wearing it together with blue sapphire, diamond or hessonite, the gems of planets held unfriendly to the Sun |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Sunday, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: ring finger. Metal: gold or copper |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | A short trial is optional. Remove it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: vitality, authority, standing and the father figure |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask [your astrologer](/consult). Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic, treatment and weight |
| Treatment in words | "Heated" and "glass-filled" are very different in value and care |
| Origin as the report's opinion | Not the seller's say-so |
| Weight in carats | Compare price per carat, not per ratti |
