## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Citrine, Sunela (a trade name) |
| Mineral | Quartz (silicon dioxide). Yellow from iron |
| Hardness | 7 on the Mohs scale |
| Traditional association | Jupiter (Guru), in practice as a lower-cost alternative to yellow sapphire |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Brazil | The main source, from Rio Grande do Sul |
| Zambia, Madagascar | Further sources |
| Bolivia | Source of ametrine (amethyst and citrine in one stone) |
| Not set by the name | Deep orange "Madeira" colour is usually heated amethyst |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Pale yellow. Rare |
| Heated amethyst | Most citrine on the market. Heat gives yellow to orange. Normal if stated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown citrine exists |
| Look-alikes | Yellow glass and yellow topaz |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice uses citrine as a lower-cost alternative to yellow sapphire for Jupiter. It is a different stone |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Brihaspati, teacher of the gods | Brihaspati, son of the sage Angiras, is the guru of the devas and counsellor of Indra. He stands for wisdom, teaching and fortune |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Thursday, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: index finger. Metal: gold |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | A short trial is optional. Remove it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: wisdom, teaching, children and good fortune |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| A substitute | Held to act milder than the main gem. Practitioners differ on how much |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic and any heating |
| Species in words | Quartz (citrine), not sapphire or topaz |
| What "Sunela" means | Ask in writing. It is a trade name |
