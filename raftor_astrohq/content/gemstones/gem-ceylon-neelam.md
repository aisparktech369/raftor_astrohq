## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Neelam, blue sapphire. "Ceylon" is the old name of Sri Lanka |
| Mineral | Corundum (aluminium oxide). Blue from iron and titanium |
| Hardness | 9 on the Mohs scale. Durable for daily wear |
| Traditional association | Saturn (Shani) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Sri Lanka (Ratnapura) | Often lighter and brighter blue. A long-standing source |
| Myanmar | Rich, deep blue. A historic source |
| Kashmir | Velvety blue. Mines gave very little and mostly long ago, so a "Kashmir" claim needs strong proof |
| Madagascar | A large modern source of varied quality |
| Thailand, Australia | Darker, inky blue |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, heated | Very common. Heating improves colour. It must be stated |
| Diffused | Colour added at the surface by heating with other elements. Must be stated and sells for much less |
| Glass-filled | Cracks filled with glass. Must be stated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown sapphire. Same chemistry, not natural |
| Look-alikes | Blue topaz, iolite and glass are different materials |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links blue sapphire with Saturn, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Practice | Many practitioners advise a trial period and an astrologer's guidance before wearing it continuously. This is practice, not a rule of a text cited here |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic, treatment and weight |
| Origin as the report's opinion | A "Ceylon" or "Kashmir" tag is only a claim |
| Treatment in words | Heated, diffused and filled differ greatly in value |
| Weight in carats | Compare price per carat, not per ratti |
