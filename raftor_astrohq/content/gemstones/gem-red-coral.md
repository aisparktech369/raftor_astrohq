## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Red coral, Moonga, Praval |
| Mineral | Calcium carbonate formed by marine coral (Corallium). An organic gem |
| Hardness | 3.5 on the Mohs scale. Soft |
| Traditional association | Mars (Mangal) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Mediterranean | The historic source of Corallium rubrum, including Italy and Spain |
| Japan, Taiwan | Pacific sources of deeper red and pink coral |
| Sourcing | Coral is regulated in several countries. Ask where and how it was gathered |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Natural colour, cut and polished |
| Dyed or bleached | Colour added to pale or other coral. Must be stated |
| Impregnated | Wax or resin to harden or smooth. Must be stated |
| Reconstituted | Coral powder pressed with resin. Not natural coral |
| Imitation | Glass, plastic or dyed bamboo coral sold as red coral |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links red coral with Mars, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: energy, courage and property |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask [your astrologer](/consult). Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural coral, the species group and any dye or resin |
| Treatment in words | Dyed, bleached or impregnated |
| Sourcing | Where it came from and that the trade is lawful |
| Care | Soft and sensitive to acid, perfume and sweat |
