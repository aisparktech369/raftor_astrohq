## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Cat's eye, Lehsunia, Vaidurya |
| Mineral | Chrysoberyl (variety cymophane). The line comes from fine needle-like inclusions |
| Hardness | 8.5 on the Mohs scale. Very durable |
| Traditional association | Ketu |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Sri Lanka | The best-known source. Fine honey-coloured stones with a sharp line |
| Brazil | A large source of many colours |
| India | Smaller sources, varied quality |
| Tanzania, Madagascar | Modern sources |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Most chrysoberyl cat's eye is not treated |
| Quartz cat's eye | A different, cheaper mineral. It must be named as quartz |
| Glass and fibre | Imitations made of fibre-optic glass show a very sharp line |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown cat's eye is rare |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice links cat's eye with Ketu, and it is one of the nine gems of the Navaratna |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Svarbhanu and the nectar | In the churning of the ocean the demon Svarbhanu drank the nectar in disguise and Vishnu severed his head. The head is Rahu and the body became Ketu, which stands for detachment and liberation |

## How astrologers decide

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| When astrologers consider it | When the planet is judged friendly to your birth chart (by the ascendant) and weak or badly placed, or when its major period is running. It is decided from the chart, not from the sun sign alone |
| The rule behind it | A gem is held to strengthen its planet, whatever the planet signifies for you. A planet that is difficult for your chart is usually not strengthened |
| Cautions in practice | Ketu stands for detachment, and many astrologers advise a chart check before wearing |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Tuesday or Thursday, by practice, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: middle finger. Metal: silver or panchdhatu |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | Many astrologers advise a short trial for strong gems, and removing it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: detachment, insight and spiritual search |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts hold that a natural, clean stone of good colour, free of cracks and spots, is the one worth wearing. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as |
| Weight | Set by your astrologer from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| The species in the report | It should say chrysoberyl cat's eye. Trade rules say "cat's eye" with no other name means chrysoberyl |
| A centred, sharp line | The line should sit in the middle and stay sharp when the stone is turned |
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic and weight |
| Weight in carats | Compare price per carat, not per ratti |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is tiger eye the same as cat's eye?</summary><p>No. Tiger eye is a quartz and is much cheaper. It is sometimes used in practice as a lower-cost alternative.</p></details>

<details><summary>What is the milk and honey effect?</summary><p>On a good cat's eye the line divides the stone into a light and a dark half when lit from one side.</p></details>
