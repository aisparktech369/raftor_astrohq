## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Agate (Hakik) and Sulemani hakik |
| Mineral | Chalcedony, a fine-grained quartz (silicon dioxide) |
| Hardness | 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale |
| Traditional association | Uses in practice vary. Ask [your astrologer](/consult) |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| India (Gujarat) | A long-standing home of agate cutting and beadmaking |
| Brazil, Uruguay | The largest sources of agate |
| Madagascar | A modern source |
| Yemen | The old trade origin of the name Hakik (Arabic aqiq) |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural colour | Browns, greys and whites as they form |
| Dyed | Black, blue, green and red agate is usually dyed. Normal if stated |
| Sulemani hakik | Black with white or grey bands. Usually dyed |
| Look-alikes | Dyed glass, resin and plastic |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Agate is not one of the nine Navaratna. Its use in practice varies by family and astrologer |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Old craft | Beads of chalcedony, the family of agate and carnelian, were made in the Indus Valley, and the Khambhat (Cambay) craft in Gujarat continues it |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | No classical themes are attached to this stone. Ask your astrologer what, if anything, it is advised for |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by your astrologer from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.
