## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Amethyst, Jamunia |
| Mineral | Quartz (silicon dioxide). Purple from iron and natural radiation |
| Hardness | 7 on the Mohs scale |
| Traditional association | Saturn (Shani), in practice as a lower-cost alternative to blue sapphire |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Brazil, Uruguay | The largest sources, with deep purple from Uruguay |
| Zambia | Deep purple stones |
| Bolivia, Madagascar | Further sources |
| Russia (Urals) | A historic source |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Natural colour, cut and polished |
| Heated | Heat turns amethyst yellow to orange, sold as citrine. Pale stones may be heated or irradiated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown amethyst is common and hard to tell by eye |
| Look-alikes | Purple glass and dyed quartz |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice uses amethyst as a lower-cost alternative to blue sapphire for Saturn. It is a different stone |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Shani, son of Surya and Chhaya | Shani is the son of the Sun and his shadow-wife Chhaya, and brother of Yama. One tale (Brahma Vaivarta Purana) has his glance fall on the infant Ganesha. He is the slow planet and the giver of results of past actions |
| Western lore | The name comes from the Greek amethystos, "not drunk". A Greek tale says the stone protected its wearer from intoxication |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Saturday, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: middle finger. Metal: silver or panchdhatu |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | Many astrologers advise a short trial for strong gems, and removing it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: discipline, delay, hard work and long effort |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| A substitute | Held to act milder than the main gem. Practitioners differ on how much |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| An independent laboratory report | States natural or synthetic and any heating |
| Colour zoning | Natural amethyst often shows uneven colour bands |
| Weight in carats | Compare price per carat |

## Questions people ask

<details><summary>Is amethyst the same as Neelam?</summary><p>No. Neelam is blue sapphire. Amethyst is purple quartz and costs much less.</p></details>

<details><summary>Does heat change amethyst?</summary><p>Yes. Heat can turn it yellow, which is sold as citrine.</p></details>
