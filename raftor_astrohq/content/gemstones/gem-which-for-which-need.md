## How to use this guide

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does it do? | It starts from what you want help with and names the planets the tradition looks at, then the gem, the Rudraksha bead and the pooja linked to them |
| Does a gem, bead or pooja bring the result? | No result is promised. Everything here is what the tradition says |
| Who decides? | A qualified astrologer reads your birth chart first and says which planet matters for you |
| Can I get all three kinds of help? | Yes. A gem, a Rudraksha bead and a pooja are three different ways, and you can ask about any one of them or all |

## Sade Sati

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planet the tradition looks at | Saturn (Shani), moving over the Moon sign and the signs beside it, for about seven and a half years |
| Gem | Blue sapphire (Neelam), only after an astrologer has checked the chart and a trial. Amethyst is read as a milder alternative in some lineages |
| Rudraksha | 7 mukhi and 14 mukhi are the beads popular practice links with Saturn |
| Pooja | Sade sati shanti pooja and Shani graha shanti havan |
| Day and deity | Saturday. Shani and Hanuman |
| Note | The tradition treats it as a long period of effort and discipline, not only of trouble. An astrologer reads how it sits in your chart |

## Shani Dhaiya (small Sade Sati)

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planet the tradition looks at | Saturn, in the fourth or eighth house from the Moon, for about two and a half years |
| Gem | The same as for Saturn: blue sapphire (Neelam), only as an astrologer advises |
| Rudraksha | 7 mukhi and 14 mukhi |
| Pooja | Shani graha shanti havan |

## Career, job, promotion and work problems

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | The Sun for position and authority, Saturn for steady work and promotion, Mercury for skill and communication. The chart shows which of them matters |
| Gem | Ruby (Sun), emerald (Mercury), blue sapphire (Saturn, with care) |
| Rudraksha | 1 mukhi or 12 mukhi (Sun), 4 mukhi (Mercury), 7 mukhi (Saturn) |
| Pooja | Ganesh karya-siddhi pooja for obstacles in work, Karya-badha nivaran pooja, Surya graha shanti pooja |
| Note | Which planet matters for a job differs from chart to chart. An astrologer decides |

## Business, shop, trade and profit

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | Mercury for trade, Jupiter for advice and growth, Venus for dealings with customers |
| Gem | Emerald (Mercury), yellow sapphire (Jupiter) |
| Rudraksha | 4 mukhi (Mercury), 5 mukhi (Jupiter), Ganesh mukhi |
| Pooja | Ganesh karya-siddhi pooja, Kuber-Lakshmi pooja, Budh graha shanti pooja. For a new shop, the Dukan opening pooja where it is offered |

## Marriage, love, relationships and finding a partner

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | Venus for love and marriage, Jupiter (for women, the husband), Mars when Mangal dosha is said |
| Gem | Diamond (Venus), with white sapphire, white topaz or zircon as substitutes. Yellow sapphire (Jupiter) |
| Rudraksha | 6 mukhi (Venus), 5 mukhi (Jupiter) |
| Pooja | Gauri-Shankar vivah kamna pooja, Shukra graha shanti pooja, Guru graha shanti pooja |

## Money, wealth, savings and debt

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | Jupiter for wealth and good fortune, Venus for comfort, Mercury for trade |
| Gem | Yellow sapphire (Jupiter), emerald (Mercury) |
| Rudraksha | 5 mukhi (Jupiter), 6 mukhi (Venus), Ganesh mukhi |
| Pooja | Kuber-Lakshmi pooja, Rin-harta Ganesh pooja for debt, Dhan-vapasi Ganesh pooja for money held back |
| Note | No pooja replaces a plan for income and debt. Speak to a financial adviser for money decisions |

## Health worries

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | The Sun for vitality, the Moon for the mind, Mars for energy, Saturn for long illness |
| Gem | Ruby (Sun), pearl (Moon), red coral (Mars) |
| Rudraksha | 5 mukhi is worn in many traditions for general wellbeing. 1 mukhi (Sun), 2 mukhi (Moon) |
| Pooja | Surya graha shanti pooja, Chandra graha shanti pooja, Mangal graha shanti pooja |
| Note | A gem, a bead or a pooja is not a treatment. For any health concern, see a doctor |

## Mangal dosha

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planet the tradition looks at | Mars (Mangala), when it stands in certain houses of the chart. Astrologers differ on how much weight it deserves |
| Gem | Red coral (Moonga), only as an astrologer advises |
| Rudraksha | 3 mukhi |
| Pooja | Mangal dosh nivaran, Mangal graha shanti pooja |

## Kaal Sarp dosha

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Planets the tradition looks at | Rahu and Ketu, when all the planets lie between them. Astrologers differ on whether this dosha is real |
| Gem | Hessonite (Gomed) for Rahu and cat's eye (Lehsunia) for Ketu, only as an astrologer advises |
| Rudraksha | 8 mukhi (Rahu), 9 mukhi (Ketu) |
| Pooja | Kaal Sarp dosh nivaran, Rahu graha shanti pooja, Ketu graha shanti pooja |

## When to talk to a live astrologer

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| You do not know which planet matters in your chart | Only a reading of the birth chart says. This page cannot |
| You are about to buy a costly stone such as a blue sapphire | It is a strong stone, and astrologers usually advise a trial first |
| The need is personal or serious: a decision, a worry, a health matter | A one-to-one session with an astrologer can look at your case. It is a paid session |
