## How to read this page

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is this? | A map of what the tradition links to each planet: its gem, its Rudraksha bead and its pooja |
| Does wearing the gem or bead bring a result? | No result is promised. These are traditional associations, not tested properties |
| Who decides which planet matters for you? | A qualified astrologer, from your birth chart. A planet that is helpful in one chart can be unhelpful in another |
| Is a pooja or a stone a substitute for a doctor, a lawyer or an adviser? | No |

## Sun (Surya): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Ruby (Manikya) |
| Rudraksha | 1 mukhi and 12 mukhi. The 12 mukhi is linked with the twelve Adityas, the forms of the Sun |
| Day | Sunday |
| Deity | Surya |
| Pooja | Surya graha shanti pooja |
| The Sun stands for | Authority, vitality, the father, a sense of self |

## Moon (Chandra): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Pearl (Moti). Moonstone is a traditional alternative |
| Rudraksha | 2 mukhi, linked with Ardhanarishvara |
| Day | Monday |
| Deity | Shiva and Chandra |
| Pooja | Chandra graha shanti pooja |
| The Moon stands for | The mind, mood, the mother, sleep and calm |

## Mars (Mangala): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Red coral (Moonga) |
| Rudraksha | 3 mukhi, linked with Agni, the fire |
| Day | Tuesday |
| Deity | Hanuman and Kartikeya |
| Pooja | Mangal graha shanti pooja. For Mangal dosha, Mangal dosh nivaran |
| Mars stands for | Energy, courage, land and property, brothers |

## Mercury (Budha): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Emerald (Panna) |
| Rudraksha | 4 mukhi, linked with Brahma, the giver of knowledge |
| Day | Wednesday |
| Deity | Vishnu and Ganesha |
| Pooja | Budh graha shanti pooja |
| Mercury stands for | Speech, learning, trade, analysis and communication |

## Jupiter (Guru): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Yellow sapphire (Pukhraj) |
| Rudraksha | 5 mukhi, linked with Kalagni Rudra. It is the most common bead and is worn in many traditions |
| Day | Thursday |
| Deity | Brihaspati and Dakshinamurti |
| Pooja | Guru graha shanti pooja |
| Jupiter stands for | Wisdom, teachers, children, wealth and good fortune |

## Venus (Shukra): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Diamond (Heera). The tradition lists white sapphire, white topaz and zircon as lower-cost substitutes |
| Rudraksha | 6 mukhi, linked with Kartikeya |
| Day | Friday |
| Deity | Lakshmi |
| Pooja | Shukra graha shanti pooja |
| Venus stands for | Love, marriage, comfort, the arts and luxury |

## Saturn (Shani): gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Blue sapphire (Neelam). It is a strong stone: astrologers usually ask for a trial period first. Amethyst is read as a milder alternative in some lineages |
| Rudraksha | 7 mukhi and 14 mukhi are the beads popular practice links with Saturn |
| Day | Saturday |
| Deity | Shani and Hanuman |
| Pooja | Shani graha shanti havan. For Sade Sati, Sade sati shanti pooja |
| Saturn stands for | Discipline, delay, long effort, service and the results of past action |

## Rahu: gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Hessonite (Gomed) |
| Rudraksha | 8 mukhi, linked with Ganesha |
| Day | Saturday |
| Deity | Durga |
| Pooja | Rahu graha shanti pooja. For Kaal Sarp dosha, Kaal Sarp dosh nivaran |
| Rahu stands for | Ambition, foreign matters, sudden change and confusion |

## Ketu: gem, Rudraksha and pooja

| Link | What the tradition says |
|---|---|
| Gem | Cat's eye (Lehsunia) |
| Rudraksha | 9 mukhi, linked with Durga |
| Day | Tuesday or Thursday |
| Deity | Ganesha |
| Pooja | Ketu graha shanti pooja. For Kaal Sarp dosha, Kaal Sarp dosh nivaran |
| Ketu stands for | Detachment, spiritual search, loss and endings |

## All nine together

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there one pooja for all nine planets? | Yes. Navagraha shanti pooja is the pooja for the nine planets together |
| Should I wear all nine gems? | No. The tradition does not ask for that. One planet at a time, as advised for your chart |
