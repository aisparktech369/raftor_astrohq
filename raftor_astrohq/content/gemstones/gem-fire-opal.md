## At a glance

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Fire opal and opal, including Australian fire opal |
| Mineral | Opal (hydrated silica). Not crystalline |
| Hardness | 5.5 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale, with a water content |
| Traditional association | Venus (Shukra), in practice as a lower-cost alternative to diamond |
| Not promised | Wealth, health, or a guaranteed result |

## Where it comes from

| Origin | What to know |
|---|---|
| Australia | About all the world's precious opal, with play-of-colour |
| Mexico | The main source of transparent fire opal in orange and red |
| Ethiopia | A large modern source of opal |
| Not set by the name | "Australian fire opal" is a trade name. Ask what the report says |

## Natural, treated, or made

| Kind | What it means |
|---|---|
| Natural, untreated | Cut and polished solid opal |
| Doublet or triplet | Thin opal glued to a base. Must be sold as such |
| Treated | Smoked, sugar-treated or impregnated to darken or stabilise. Must be stated |
| Synthetic | Lab-grown opal exists |
| Imitation | Plastic and glass |

## Meaning in the tradition

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Jyotish practice uses opal as a lower-cost alternative to diamond for Venus. It is a different stone |
| Texts | The Garuda Purana and Varahamihira's Brihat Samhita describe qualities and faults of gems |
| Status of the claim | The planetary link is a matter of religious tradition. It is not a scientific property of the stone or a promise of any result |

## Mythology and lore

| Story | What is told |
|---|---|
| Shukracharya, teacher of the asuras | Shukracharya guided the asuras and held the Sanjivani vidya that revives the dead. In the story of Vamana at King Bali's sacrifice, he lost an eye to a blade of kusha grass |

## When and how to start

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best day to start | Friday, in the hour (hora) of the planet, in the bright half of the lunar month (Shukla paksha) |
| Before wearing | Wash in raw milk or Ganga water, offer flowers, and chant the planet's mantra 108 times. This is practice and differs by lineage |
| Hand and finger | The right hand is common. Finger: middle finger (ring finger in some practice). Metal: silver or platinum |
| Contact | Practice asks that the stone touches the skin |
| Trial | A short trial is optional. Remove it if you feel uneasy |

## Effectiveness: what to expect

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| What the tradition says | Results are described through the planet's themes: relationships, comfort and the arts |
| How long | No fixed period in the cited texts. Practitioners commonly speak of watching for several weeks to a few months |
| The evidence | There is no scientific evidence that a gemstone changes events. Reported effects are personal and cannot be promised |
| Quality | The gem texts warn against faults such as cracks, cavities and cloudiness, and prize good colour. A lab-made, dyed or glass-filled stone is not the stone it is sold as. How a natural stone looks (size, depth of colour, cleanness) is a matter of finish |
| Weight | Set by [your astrologer](/consult) from the chart. As a buyer, compare the price per carat |
| A substitute | Held to act milder than the main gem. Practitioners differ on how much |
| If unsure | Stop wearing it if you feel uneasy and ask your astrologer. Do not change medical care or major decisions on the strength of a gem |

The stories and rules above are traditions, told as they are told. They are not claims of results.

## What to ask for

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|

| Solid, doublet or triplet | In words |
| Care | Avoid heat, dry air, sudden changes and ultrasonic cleaners. Opal can crack |
